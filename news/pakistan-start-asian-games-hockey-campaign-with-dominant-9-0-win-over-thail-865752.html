Pakistan Start Asian Games Hockey Campaign with Dominant 9-0 Win Over Thailand

By Umer Javed | 21 September 2026 | 6 min read
Photo: Sóc Năng Động / Pexels

Pakistan's men's hockey team thrashed Thailand 9-0 in their opening Asian Games pool B match on Sunday. Ahmed Nadeem scored a hat-trick while Sufyan Khan struck twice from penalty corners as Pakistan dominated from start to finish.

Pakistan Cruise Past Thailand in Opening Match

Pakistan's men's hockey team launched their Asian Games campaign with a commanding 9-0 victory over Thailand in a pool B encounter at the Gifu Prefectural Green Stadium on Sunday. The match turned into a one-sided affair from the very beginning, as Pakistan asserted their authority early and never looked back. The result puts the team in a strong position heading deeper into the tournament, and the performance will be seen as an encouraging start to their continental campaign.

The Pakistani players were clinical in front of goal and disciplined in defence, leaving Thailand with little room to breathe on the ball. The visitors to the ground will have been encouraged by the level of play on display, which combined attacking flair with solid defensive organisation. Thailand,, appeared overwhelmed by the pace and intensity that Pakistan brought to the contest from the opening minutes.

Moeen Shakeel and Ammad Butt Set the Tone

Moeen Shakeel opened the scoring in the eighth minute to break the deadlock and give Pakistan the lead when it mattered most. His goal injected momentum into the side and set the tone for what followed. Ammad Butt doubled the lead just four minutes later, putting the game well beyond Thailand's reach at an early stage. The rapid succession of goals forced the Thai side into a reactive mode, which played directly into Pakistan's hands.

With two goals inside the first twelve minutes, Pakistan seized control of the contest and never surrendered their hold on the match. The early pressure paid off and the team continued to push for further goals rather than sitting back. This proactive approach frustrated Thailand and gave the Pakistani supporters plenty to cheer from the outset.

Ahmed Nadeem's Hat-Trick and Sufyan Khan's Penalty Corner Precision

Ahmed Nadeem got his first goal in the 20th minute, continuing Pakistan's steady supply of scores throughout the match. Nadeem would go on to complete a hat-trick, underlining his central role in the team's attacking plans for the tournament. Sufyan Khan also played a major part, striking twice from penalty corners to add further goals to Pakistan's tally. His ability from set pieces gave Pakistan a reliable weapon that Thailand found extremely difficult to neutralise.

Nadeem's three goals and Sufyan Khan's two strikes made them the standout performers on the day. Their contributions ensured that Pakistan's attack remained potent across all phases of the game. The pair's composure and execution under pressure will be viewed positively by the coaching staff as the team looks to maintain this standard in the matches ahead. The partnership between the two in attack provided the finishing touch that Thailand simply could not handle.

A Team in Complete Control

Thailand attempted to regroup during the interval, but they found no answers once the second half got underway. Pakistan continued to press with the same intensity that characterised the first half, and the scoreline continued to grow in their favour. The green and white shirts moved the ball with purpose and created multiple scoring opportunities that tested the Thai defence repeatedly.

According to reports, the margin of the victory reflected the significant gap in class between the two sides on this occasion. Pakistan's players showed excellent fitness levels and maintained their tempo from the first whistle through to the final whistle. The discipline and organisation displayed across all lines of the team pointed to thorough preparation and a clear game plan that was executed with conviction.

Pool B and the Road Ahead

The Asian Games hockey tournament features several pools, with teams competing to secure a place in the knockout stages. Pool B presents Pakistan with a route that they will be confident of dealing with successfully based on the performance against Thailand. The victory ensures that the team sits in a strong position in the group standings going into their next fixture.

Every match in the group stage carries significance, as teams jostle for favourable positions ahead of the elimination rounds. Pakistan will be aware that while the Thailand result was emphatic, the level of competition will increase as they face stronger opponents in the pool. Maintaining focus and intensity across all matches will be essential if the team is to advance deep into the tournament.

Pakistan's Proud Hockey Heritage

Pakistan has a storied history in field hockey that stretches back several decades. The country has won the Hockey World Cup on four occasions and has claimed Olympic gold medals in the sport, establishing itself as one of the all-time greats. At the Asian Games, Pakistan has traditionally been among the medal contenders, with a record that includes multiple gold medals over the years. The current squad carries the weight of that proud legacy, and performances like the one against Thailand will be seen as a positive signal.

The Asian Games provide an important stage for Pakistani athletes across all disciplines. Hockey, in particular, holds a special place in the country's sporting culture and continues to inspire passionate support from fans at home and abroad. A convincing opening win of this nature is sure to generate optimism among the Pakistani sporting community and strengthen the belief that the team can compete for honours at this event.

What Lies Ahead for the Green and White

With this opening victory secured, Pakistan will look to build on the positive momentum as they prepare for their next pool B match. The coaching staff will take encouragement from the attacking output and the defensive solidity that the team displayed against Thailand. Keeping this level of performance consistent will be the central challenge in the coming days as the tournament progresses.

The competition ahead is expected to be fierce, with several strong teams vying for top honours. Pakistan's path to the knockout stages should remain manageable based on the quality shown in this opening match, but every fixture will demand full commitment and concentration. The players and support staff will understand that one dominant performance does not guarantee success across the full span of the tournament.

For now, the attention remains on building on this promising start. The 9-0 scoreline against Thailand sends a clear message about Pakistan's ambitions at these Asian Games. If the team can carry this form forward through the group stage and beyond, there is every reason to believe they will be serious contenders for a place on the podium.
