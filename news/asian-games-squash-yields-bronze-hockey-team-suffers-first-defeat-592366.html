Asian Games: Squash yields bronze, hockey team suffers first defeat

By Umer Javed | 26 September 2026 | 6 min read
Photo: Weezy Mie / Pexels

Pakistan's squash semi-final hopes concluded with two bronze medals on Saturday, while the national hockey team's unbeaten streak was broken by Malaysia. This marked a challenging day for the Pakistani contingent at the Asian Games, with both boxers also exiting their respective competitions.

Pakistan experienced a day of mixed fortunes at the Asian Games, as the country's squash contingent secured two bronze medals following semi-final defeats, while the national hockey team's undefeated run came to an end against Malaysia. The day also saw both Pakistani boxers eliminated from their respective categories, leaving the nation reflecting on a bruising series of outcomes across various disciplines.

The squash events, held at the Nagoya Kinjo Futo Arena, concluded for Pakistan with Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan each settling for bronze. Both athletes faced formidable opponents in their semi-final clashes and ultimately fell in straight games, marking the end of their individual medal pursuits.

Noor Zaman, considered Pakistan’s top-ranked player in the tournament, engaged in a highly competitive encounter against top seed Eain Yow Ng. The opening game was particularly gripping, with both players demonstrating exceptional skill and determination. They traded points evenly, reaching a 5-5 deadlock before Noor managed to edge ahead, establishing a 7-5 lead. However, the Malaysian opponent showcased resilience and skill, halting Noor's momentum and later launching a strong comeback.

Despite Noor Zaman's initial lead and spirited performance, Eain Yow Ng managed to regain control, eventually winning the opening game and later the match in straight games. This result, while ending Noor's gold or silver medal aspirations, automatically awarded him a bronze medal, a standard practice in squash tournaments where losing semi-finalists are not required to play a third-place match.

Similarly, Mohammad Ashab Irfan also faced a challenging semi-final match, where he too was defeated in straight games. His performance throughout the tournament had been commendable, leading him to the semi-final stage, a significant achievement in itself. The bronze medal secured by Irfan stands as his dedication and skill on the international stage.

These two bronze medals from the squash court represent a valuable addition to Pakistan's overall medal tally at the Asian Games. Squash has historically been a strong suit for Pakistan, with a rich legacy of world champions and top-tier players. The current generation of players continues to carry this tradition, and securing medals at a prestigious event like the Asian Games is an important step in maintaining Pakistan's presence in the sport globally.

The Asian Games squash competition draws talent from across the continent, making every medal a hard-earned achievement. For Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan, reaching the semi-finals and securing bronze medals demonstrates their competitive spirit and potential for future successes. Their journey through the tournament, overcoming various opponents, highlights the intensity and skill required to compete at this level.

Hockey Team's Unbeaten Run Ends Against Malaysia

Adding to the day's challenges, Pakistan's national hockey team suffered its first defeat of the tournament, falling to Malaysia. This loss marked a significant turn after what had been an unbeaten run for the team in their earlier matches. The defeat against a strong Malaysian side introduces a new dynamic into their tournament progression, necessitating a reassessment of strategies as the competition advances.

Pakistan's hockey team traditionally carries immense expectations into international tournaments, given the nation's illustrious history in the sport, including multiple Olympic and World Cup titles. The team's performance leading up to this match had instilled confidence among supporters, making the defeat to Malaysia a notable setback.

Field hockey in Asia is highly competitive, with several nations consistently fielding strong teams capable of challenging for top honors. Malaysia has emerged as a formidable opponent in recent years, often engaging in closely contested matches with traditional powerhouses like Pakistan, India, and South Korea. This particular match was a demonstration of that competitive.

The outcome of this match will undoubtedly prompt the team management and players to analyze their performance, identify areas for improvement, and prepare for subsequent important matches. In a multi-team tournament format, every match carries weight, and an early defeat, particularly after an unbeaten start, can serve as a wake-up call, reinforcing the need for consistent performance at the highest level.

The journey through the Asian Games hockey tournament is a marathon, not a sprint. Teams must maintain peak physical condition and tactical discipline through several matches against varied opponents. The defeat, while disappointing, provides an opportunity for the team to regroup and refocus on their objectives for the remainder of the competition.

Boxers Ousted: A Challenging Day for the Contingent

Further contributing to the day's difficulties, both Pakistani boxers competing at the Asian Games were eliminated from their respective categories. This development means Pakistan's medal hopes in boxing have concluded, adding to the country's tally of losses on what was described as a bruising day for the national contingent.

Boxing is a sport that demands extraordinary physical conditioning, mental fortitude, and technical prowess. Competitors at the Asian Games represent the pinnacle of amateur boxing in their respective countries, making every bout a fierce contest. The path to a medal in boxing is arduous, requiring athletes to overcome multiple opponents through a series of demanding fights.

The exit of both boxers highlights the intense competition prevalent in the sport at this level. Athletes train for years, dedicating themselves to mastering their craft, and facing opponents who possess similar levels of commitment and skill. While the results may be disappointing, the participation of Pakistani boxers at such a prestigious event is important for the development and exposure of the sport within the country.

For young Pakistani athletes aspiring to compete internationally, the experiences of their national representatives at events like the Asian Games provide valuable lessons and inspiration. The challenges faced by the boxers on this particular day are part of the broader journey of athletic development and competition on the global stage.

Counting the Losses: A Bruising Day at the Asian Games

The collective results from squash, hockey, and boxing painted a picture of a challenging day for Pakistan at the Asian Games. The initial hopes associated with semi-final appearances and an unbeaten streak in hockey gave way to a series of defeats, leaving the country counting its losses across these key sports.

Multi-sport events like the Asian Games are inherently unpredictable, with moments of triumph often interspersed with periods of setback. Athletes dedicate years of their lives to preparing for these competitions, and the pressure to perform for their nation is immense. Each medal, regardless of its color, is a symbol of immense effort, sacrifice, and national pride.

While the immediate outcomes on this particular Saturday may have been disappointing in certain respects, the broader narrative of Pakistan's participation in the Asian Games continues. The experience gained by athletes competing at such a high level is invaluable, contributing to their growth and providing important insights for future international engagements.

The spirit of competition, the pursuit of excellence, and the honor of representing one's country remain vital. As the Asian Games progress, the Pakistani contingent will continue to strive for success in remaining events, aiming to build on the lessons learned and celebrate any further achievements that come their way. The journey of an athlete is one of continuous learning, resilience, and unwavering commitment to their sport and nation.
