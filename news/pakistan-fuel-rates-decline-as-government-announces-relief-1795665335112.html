Specific Price Reductions for Petrol and Diesel

The extent of the relief varies slightly between the two primary petroleum products utilized nationwide. Geo News reported that the petrol price has dropped by a specific monetary amount per litre. Specifically, the rate has been cut by four rupees. This adjustment affects motorists who use petrol for their daily personal transport and smaller commercial vehicles operating within urban areas.

In parallel, the adjustment for diesel is slightly higher according to the reported figures. Geo News indicated that the diesel price has decreased by five rupees per litre. This particular decrease brings the overall cost of diesel below three hundred and fifty rupees for the first time in four months, as highlighted by Dawn. Dunya News also confirmed these figures, noting the four rupee cut for petrol and the five rupee reduction for diesel. Such specific reductions are tracked carefully by logistics providers.
