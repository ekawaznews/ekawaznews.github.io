Diplomatic Arrival in the Capital

The Foreign Minister of Bahrain, Abdullatif bin Rashid Al Zayani, arrived in Islamabad on Wednesday evening for a scheduled two-day diplomatic mission. Dawn reported that the visit was arranged following a formal invitation extended by the Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister of Pakistan, Ishaq Dar. Upon his landing, the visiting official was greeted by the Secretary of the Economic Affairs Division, Muhammad Humair Karim, and the Additional Secretary for the Middle East, Zahid Hussain. This visit is intended to facilitate high-level discussions between the two nations across multiple sectors.

According to the Foreign Office, the primary objective of the trip is to co-chair the third session of the Joint Ministerial Commission. This platform serves as a formal mechanism for both countries to evaluate their existing partnership and explore new avenues for collaboration. The government views this diplomatic interaction as a way to affirm the shared commitment of both nations to improve their bilateral cooperation. By reviewing the entire spectrum of their relationship, the two countries seek to identify areas where their interests align and where they can provide mutual support.

For the people of Pakistan, such diplomatic visits are often viewed as a way to maintain stable international connections. The Foreign Office noted that the presence of the Bahraini delegation in Islamabad acts as a sign of the brotherly connection shared by the two nations.
