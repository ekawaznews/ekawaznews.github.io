The forecasted weather conditions across different cities will directly influence transportation and routine commuting. Drivers in foggy areas are advised to exercise caution and maintain lower speeds to prevent accidents. Impact on Farming and Agriculture

Drizzle and rainfall expected in northern and western areas can provide necessary moisture for certain crops, though excessive downpours in vulnerable farming zones might require drainage monitoring.
