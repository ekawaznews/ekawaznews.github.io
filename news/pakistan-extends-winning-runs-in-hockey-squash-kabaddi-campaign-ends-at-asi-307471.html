Pakistan Extends Winning Runs in Hockey, Squash; Kabaddi Campaign Ends at Asian Games

By Umer Javed | 24 September 2026 | 2 min read
Photo: Thegiansepillo / Pexels

Pakistan’s hockey and squash teams continued their impressive winning streaks at the Asian Games, securing further victories on Thursday. However, the day also brought disappointment for the nation as the kabaddi team concluded its campaign and boxer Maria Rind exited the tournament.

Pakistan’s contingent at the Asian Games experienced a day of mixed fortunes, with the national hockey and squash teams maintaining their winning momentum, while the kabaddi squad and boxer Maria Rind saw their respective campaigns conclude.

The hockey team delivered another commanding performance, securing a 3-0 victory over China at the Gifu Prefectural Green Stadium. This win marks their third consecutive triumph in as many matches, solidifying their position and building strong momentum in the tournament's early stages.

Hockey Team Dominates China, Continues Strong Run

The match against China was the Pakistani hockey team’s growing confidence and strategic execution. While the scoreboard eventually reflected a comfortable victory, the initial exchanges of the game presented a more balanced picture, with both sides seeking to establish an early advantage.

China, eager to assert themselves, created the first significant opportunities, earning two penalty corners in the opening quarter. However, they were unable to convert these chances into goals, a missed opportunity that often proves costly in closely contested hockey matches. Pakistan, too, had their own struggles in the early phase, squandering two penalty corners, indicating a need for sharper execution in set-piece situations.
