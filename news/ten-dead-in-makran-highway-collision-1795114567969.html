Understanding the Incident

According to Dawn, a passenger coach traveling toward Karachi collided with a liquefied petroleum gas bowser near Basol in the Ormara region. The impact of the collision caused both vehicles to erupt in flames, leading to significant loss of life and injuries to several passengers.

The passenger coach had departed from Turbat and was making its way to Karachi when the incident took place. Reports from the scene indicate that the fire spread rapidly, which prevented many passengers from escaping the burning wreckage. The local administration and emergency responders were alerted immediately to address the situation.

The route involved serves as a vital connection for travelers and cargo moving across the region. When accidents of this magnitude occur on long-distance tracks, the immediate effect is felt by all travelers who rely on the corridor for daily transit. The fire that followed the impact created extreme hazards for anyone attempting to approach the vehicles.

Local inhabitants and motorists passing through the area often find themselves facing severe delays when emergency situations block the path. The physical isolation of certain stretches along the coastline means that immediate help relies heavily on stationed patrols and nearby administrative posts.

Official Confirmation and Rescue Operations

Superintendent of Police for the Makran Coastal Highway Police, Muhammad Aslam Bangulzai, confirmed that ten individuals lost their lives in the accident. Among the deceased were a woman and a child. The official stated that the fire quickly engulfed both the bus and the gas tanker, trapping those inside the coach.

Emergency teams, including personnel from the coastal highway police and local administrative officials, arrived at the location to conduct rescue operations. The injured passengers were transported to the Ormara Health Centre to receive medical attention. Aaj English TV also reported on the incident, identifying it as a major road occurrence situated near Pasni.

Medical personnel at the health facility worked to stabilize those who suffered burn injuries from the flames. The sudden influx of patients tested the capacity of the local medical center, requiring rapid mobilization of available staff and supplies to treat the victims.

Coordinating the response required multiple agencies to work together under difficult conditions. Police officials secured the perimeter while rescue workers focused on extinguishing the remaining fire and moving the injured away from the danger zone.
