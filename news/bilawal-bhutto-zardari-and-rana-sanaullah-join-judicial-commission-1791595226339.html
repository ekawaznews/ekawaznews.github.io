Recent Developments in the Judicial Commission

The Judicial Commission of Pakistan has undergone significant changes in its composition as of Friday. Two prominent political figures have been inducted into the body to fill existing vacancies. This process involves the formal inclusion of representatives from the treasury benches of the legislature into the commission that handles key judicial matters.

According to Dawn, the individuals selected for these roles are the Chairman of the Pakistan Peoples Party, Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari, and the Adviser to the Prime Minister, Rana Sanaullah. Their appointment follows the departure of previous members who had stepped down from their positions. The transition marks a shift in the parliamentary representation within the commission as the government moves to ensure its presence is maintained.

The integration of these political figures into the commission brings new perspectives from the legislative branch directly into the judicial administration space. The Process of Appointment

The selection of these members was conducted under specific constitutional guidelines. Dawn reported that the nominations were finalized by Prime Minister Shahbaz Sharif, who serves as the leader of the House in the National Assembly. Also, the Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, Senator Ishaq Dar, participated in the process in his capacity as the leader of the house in the Senate.

These actions were taken in accordance with Article 175A (2)(vii) of the Constitution. The formal notification of these appointments was issued on Friday after receiving the necessary approval from the Chief Justice of Pakistan, Yahya Afridi. As the head of the Judicial Commission, the Chief Justice holds the authority to finalize such administrative changes within the body.
