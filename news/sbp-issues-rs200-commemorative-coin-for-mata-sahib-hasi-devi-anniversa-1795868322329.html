SBP Issues Rs200 Commemorative Coin for Mata Sahib Hasi Devi Anniversary

State Bank Issues Rs200 Commemorative Coin

The State Bank of Pakistan has released a commemorative coin valued at two hundred rupees to mark the two hundredth birth anniversary of Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi. As reported by Dawn and Business Recorder, the central bank announced the currency release in an official statement issued on Friday. The coin commemorates the legacy of an important historical and spiritual figure connected to northern Sindh, offering the public a tangible piece of national currency dedicated to her memory.

Coverage by Dunya News and Investify confirmed that the commemorative currency piece entered official distribution channels immediately following the announcement. Commemorative coins issued by the central bank serve as legal tender while simultaneously acting as collectible items for numismatists and members of the public interested in historical preservation. The selection of a two hundred rupee face value aligns numerical symbolism directly with the two hundredth birth anniversary being observed.

According to Dawn, the release officially began on Friday, allowing citizens to acquire the special currency piece directly from central bank facilities. Central monetary institutions frequently utilize commemorative coinages to highlight significant historical events, community figures, and cultural milestones across the nation. This latest issuance represents part of that ongoing central banking function.

Historical Importance of Shadani Darbar and Mata Sahib Hasi Devi

Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi holds historical distinction as the fifth head of the historic Shadani Darbar, according to details provided in the central bank statement reported by Dawn. The Shadani Darbar is an established spiritual center situated in the Hayat Pitafi area within the Ghotki district of Sindh's Sukkur division. Her leadership at the sanctuary forms an recognized chapter in the spiritual history of the region.

The location of the Shadani Darbar in the Hayat Pitafi area highlights the deep historical roots of spiritual traditions in Ghotki district and the broader Sukkur division. Over generations, regional centers like Shadani Darbar have provided spiritual guidance and community spaces for local residents. Recognizing the fifth head of this historic institution through a national currency release brings regional heritage into the wider national consciousness.

By issuing a official coin on her two hundredth birth anniversary, the central bank acknowledges the historical footprint of Mata Sahib Hasi Devi. Spiritual institutions in rural Sindh have long maintained distinct historical records and leadership traditions. The commemorative coin serves to archive that historical legacy within the official numismatic history of the country.

The decision to feature a regional historical figure allows citizens across all provinces to learn about the heritage of northern Sindh. Ghotki district contains multiple sites of historical interest, and highlighting the leadership of Shadani Darbar emphasizes the institutional recognition given to historical heritage in the area. Such recognitions foster a deeper appreciation of regional history nationwide.

Physical Specifications and Minting Details

Detailed technical specifications of the commemorative coin were outlined in the central bank statement reported by Dawn. The coin is manufactured using a cupro-nickel metal alloy, consisting of seventy-five percent copper and twenty-five percent nickel. This metallic composition gives the coin a durable structure and a polished metallic appearance standard for commemorative issues.

In terms of dimensions, the coin measures thirty millimetres in diameter and weighs exactly thirteen point five grammes, as stated in the central bank details reported by Dawn. These specific physical dimensions give the piece a substantial presence compared to routine circulating coins. The precise measurements adhere to standard central bank minting guidelines for commemorative monetary pieces.

The design on the face of the coin incorporates traditional national symbols alongside specific artistic details. Dawn reported that the central face of the coin displays a waxing crescent moon and a five-pointed star positioned in the center. The crescent and star are oriented facing northwest in a rising position, creating a distinctive design pattern on the coin face.

Surrounding the central symbols along the top periphery of the coin is an official inscription reading "Islami Jamhuria Pakistan". This phrasing, inscribed above the crescent and star, clearly identifies the issuing state authority. The combination of official national emblems and clean lettering completes the visual presentation of the commemorative piece.

Distribution Protocol Through SBP Field Offices

The State Bank of Pakistan has established a clear distribution system for the public to access the new commemorative currency. Dawn reported that the Rs200 coin is available through exchange counters across all field offices of the SBP Banking Services Corporation. Distribution through these official counters officially commenced on Friday following the central bank announcement.

The SBP Banking Services Corporation maintains field offices in key cities throughout the country, acting as the operational arm for currency management and public banking services. Utilizing these established field offices ensures that citizens in various provinces can purchase or exchange currency for the commemorative coin without reliance on intermediary vendors.

By providing direct access at face value through exchange counters, the central bank ensures equitable public access. Interested collectors, historians, and citizens can obtain the coin at its official two hundred rupee value. This direct distribution model prevents speculative price increases during the initial release period.

Obtaining commemorative coins through official field office counters also provides assurance regarding authenticity. Collectors and citizens receiving coins directly from the SBP Banking Services Corporation can be confident of receiving genuine cupro-nickel mintings with exact official specifications. This process reflects standard administrative procedure for central bank commemorative releases.
