Fernando Alonso Announces Dakar Rally Return

Double Formula One champion Fernando Alonso stated that he will participate in the upcoming Dakar Rally. Al Jazeera reported that this move marks his return to the prestigious off-road race after a seven-year absence. The driver has a long history in top tier motorsport, including multiple victories across different racing categories as noted by international media outlets.

The forty-five-year-old Spanish driver announced his intention to compete in the annual event on Tuesday. According to Al Jazeera, he will work with an Aston Martin Dreadnought entry managed by engineering partner Prodrive. This announcement has drawn considerable attention from sports journalists around the world who follow high-profile athletic transitions.

Alonso previously competed in the Dakar Rally in the year 2020 driving with Toyota, where he secured a thirteenth-place finish. BBC Sport and BBC also covered his upcoming participation in the January 2027 event. Observers have noted that his prior experience in off-road endurance racing provides a foundation for this upcoming competitive endeavor.

The decision to enter the rally follows his choice to continue his career in Formula One with Aston Martin. Al Jazeera reported that he turned his thoughts toward the Dakar event after confirming his ongoing commitments with the team. Motorsport enthusiasts often discuss how elite competitors maintain performance across multiple distinct categories of racing.

Balancing Formula One and Off-Road Racing

Explaining his motivation, Alonso stated that the choice became straightforward because he wanted to embrace the challenge completely and take on both Formula One and the Dakar Rally in the same year. Al Jazeera published his remarks about his previous outing, highlighting his dedication to competing at the highest levels of motorsport.

He noted that in 2020, the Dakar event proved to be one of the most challenging and enjoyable experiences of his career. He added that he holds enormous respect for the race and the specialists who have mastered it. This perspective illustrates the demanding nature of cross-country endurance rallies compared to circuit racing.

While preparing for this venture, Alonso noted that Formula One remains his primary focus. However, he emphasized that as a racing driver, challenges of this magnitude inspire his passion for competing, and he look forward to seeing what his team can achieve. Analysts may suggest that balancing two intense racing schedules requires meticulous physical and mental preparation.

The scheduling requires careful planning from the driver throughout the year. The twenty-four-round Formula One season is scheduled to start in Bahrain in March, following testing sessions in late February. Meanwhile, testing and logistical arrangements for the January rally will demand significant time management from the entire racing squad.

Route Changes and Event Scheduling

The forty-ninth edition of the Dakar Rally will take place in Morocco from January 9 to 23. Al Jazeera noted that the event features a prologue and first stage in Portugal, after organizers moved the competition from Saudi Arabia due to security concerns. These location adjustments present new logistical factors for all participating teams and drivers.

The timing of the event sits closely alongside the demanding Formula One calendar. Alonso is currently competing in the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend, demonstrating the continuous cycle of travel and competition that defines modern elite motorsport. Team Composition and Support

For the upcoming rally, Alonso will once again partner with his compatriot Marc Coma. Al Jazeera noted that Coma is a five-time motorcycle Dakar winner who will serve as his co-driver. Their previous collaborative experience could offer strategic advantages during the grueling navigation phases of the competition.

The broader team lineup features notable international competitors. Al Jazeera reported that France's nine-time world rally champion Sebastien Loeb and Brazil's reigning world rally-raid champion Lucas Moraes will join Alonso as teammates in a three-car entry. This assembly of experienced drivers brings considerable expertise to the manufacturer's garage.

This initiative represents a significant milestone for the manufacturer. Al Jazeera reported that the Dakar event will mark Aston Martin Racing's debut appearance in the world rally-raid championship. Entering a new championship requires immense engineering resources and technical refinement.

The collaboration between professional drivers and technical engineers is vital for success in endurance racing. Teams must test vehicle durability under extreme conditions to prevent mechanical failures during stages. Observers will watch closely to see how the Prodrive-managed entry handles the terrain in Morocco and Portugal.
