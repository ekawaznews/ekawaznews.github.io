Pakistan Secures Asian Games Squash Medals as Noor, Ashab Reach Semis

By Umer Javed | 25 September 2026 | 7 min read
Photo: Anthony Yates / Pexels

Pakistan secured another medal at the Asian Games on Friday after Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan won their respective singles matches to reach the semi-finals of the squash competition. These victories guarantee at least two bronze medals for the nation, marking a significant achievement at the regional sporting event.

Pakistan secured another medal at the Asian Games on Friday after Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan won their respective singles matches to reach the semi-finals of the squash competition. This double triumph ensures at least two bronze medals for the country, bringing a renewed sense of pride and accomplishment to the national contingent participating in the prestigious regional event. The victories by the two promising players have placed Pakistan firmly in the medal rounds, building on the nation's rich legacy in the sport.

Third-seeded Noor Zaman delivered a commanding performance against Hong Kong’s Chi Hin Henry Leung, securing his place in the last four. His match, held at the Nagoya Kinjo Futo Arena, saw him pushed by his opponent, but Zaman, known for his composure, held his nerve to clinch the victory. Simultaneously, fifth-seeded Mohammad Ashab Irfan demonstrated his prowess by overcoming India’s Veer Chotrani in another hard-fought encounter at the same venue. Irfan's strong performance against the fourth-seeded Indian opponent was his skill and determination, paving the way for Pakistan to secure multiple medals in the individual squash event.

The Asian Games: A Regional Sporting Spectacle

The Asian Games stand as a monumental sporting event, bringing together athletes from across the continent in a celebration of skill, endurance, and camaraderie. Held every four years, it is the second-largest multi-sport event in the world, surpassed only by the Olympic Games. For Pakistan, participation in the Asian Games offers a vital platform for its athletes to compete against the best in Asia, providing invaluable international exposure and experience. The Games are not just about winning; they are also about fostering regional unity, cultural exchange, and inspiring a new generation of athletes.

The current edition of the Asian Games, like its predecessors, showcases a diverse range of sports, from traditional athletic disciplines to modern competitive events. Each medal won at this level is a source of immense national pride, reflecting the dedication and hard work of athletes, coaches, and supporting federations. Pakistan's performance across various sports is closely watched by its citizens, with every success contributing to the nation's morale and its standing on the international sporting. The squash competition, in particular, often draws significant attention due to Pakistan's historical association with the sport.

Pakistan's Storied Squash Legacy

Squash holds a special place in Pakistan's sporting history. For decades, Pakistan was a dominant force on the global squash circuit, producing legendary players who captivated audiences worldwide and brought numerous world titles home. This golden era established a benchmark for excellence and instilled a deep appreciation for the sport within the country. The names of Pakistani squash legends are synonymous with unparalleled skill, athleticism, and strategic brilliance, inspiring generations of aspiring athletes. Their achievements cemented Pakistan's reputation as a powerhouse in squash.

The era of Pakistani dominance in squash was characterized by a unique playing style that combined agility, power, and mental fortitude. This rich heritage has created high expectations for current players, who carry the torch of their predecessors. While the of international squash has evolved, the foundational strength and passion for the game remain deeply ingrained in Pakistan's sporting culture. The continuous effort to identify, train, and nurture new talent is the nation's commitment to reviving its past glory in the sport.

A Resurgence on the International Stage

In recent years, there has been a concerted effort to revitalise squash in Pakistan, aiming to reclaim its rightful place at the pinnacle of the sport. Various academies and training programmes have been established to unearth young talent and provide them with the necessary coaching and infrastructure to compete at the highest levels. These initiatives are designed to equip players with modern techniques and strategies, preparing them for the rigours of international competition. The focus has been on comprehensive development, encompassing physical fitness, mental resilience, and tactical acumen.

The performances of Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan at the Asian Games are strong indicators that these efforts are beginning to bear fruit. Their success signals a positive turn for Pakistani squash, offering hope for a new era of achievements. Such performances are vital not only for the players themselves but also for inspiring other young athletes across the country to pursue squash. The visibility and prestige associated with Asian Games medals can significantly boost the sport's popularity and attract greater investment in its development.

Noor Zaman's Steady Ascent

Noor Zaman, recognized as Pakistan’s top-ranked player in the draw for the Asian Games, has consistently shown promise and skill on the professional circuit. His journey to the semi-finals at the Asian Games is a reflection of his dedication and disciplined training. Facing Hong Kong’s Chi Hin Henry Leung, Zaman encountered a challenging opponent who pushed him to his limits. Despite the pressure, Zaman maintained his focus and composure, demonstrating the mental strength required to succeed at this level.

His ability to hold his nerve in critical moments allowed him to navigate the complexities of the match and emerge victorious. This win not only secured him a medal but also highlighted his status as a leading figure in Pakistani squash. The experience gained from such high-stakes matches is invaluable for his continued development and will undoubtedly serve him well in future tournaments. His performance has certainly set a high standard for the Pakistani contingent.

Ashab Irfan's Determined Performance

Mohammad Ashab Irfan's path to the semi-finals was equally impressive, marked by a determined performance against India’s Veer Chotrani. As the fifth-seeded player, Irfan faced a formidable challenge from the fourth-seeded Indian, making his victory all the more significant. The match showcased Irfan's strategic thinking and his ability to execute under pressure, allowing him to overcome a highly-rated adversary.

His strong play throughout the encounter demonstrated his readiness for top-tier competition. Defeating a higher-seeded opponent from a traditional rival nation adds another layer of significance to his achievement, boosting his confidence and signaling his potential to climb higher in the rankings. Irfan's journey through the tournament has been a display of consistent effort and growing maturity on the court.

The Road Ahead: Semi-finals and Beyond

With both Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan securing berths in the semi-finals, Pakistan is now guaranteed at least two bronze medals from the squash competition. The next challenge for both players will be their respective semi-final matches, where they will compete for a spot in the gold medal match. These upcoming contests will demand even greater focus, tactical precision, and physical endurance as they face other top-tier athletes from across Asia.

The prospect of competing for gold and silver medals is a powerful motivator, and the entire nation will be rooting for their success. Regardless of the outcome of the semi-finals, the fact that two Pakistani players have reached this stage is a commendable achievement and a significant boost for the sport domestically. Their performances have already brought immense credit to the country and highlighted the talent present within its squash ranks.

Broader Implications for Pakistani Sports

The success of Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan at the Asian Games extends beyond the squash court. Their achievements provide a much-needed morale boost for the entire Pakistani sports fraternity and the nation as a whole. In a country where sports often face funding and infrastructure challenges, such victories serve as powerful reminders of the potential that exists within its youth. They inspire countless young individuals to pursue their sporting dreams and work towards representing their country on international platforms.

These medals also bring positive attention to Pakistan on the global stage, showcasing the country's capabilities in sports. They can stimulate greater investment in sports development, encourage corporate sponsorships, and foster a more strong sporting culture. The sense of national pride that emanates from these successes is immeasurable, uniting people and celebrating shared accomplishments. The entire nation eagerly anticipates the next phase of the squash competition, hopeful for even greater triumphs.
