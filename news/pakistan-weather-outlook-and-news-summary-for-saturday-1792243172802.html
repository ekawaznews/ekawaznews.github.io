Balochistan Earthquake Report

In other news reported today, a seismic event shook Balochistan. According to Dawn, a magnitude 5.9 earthquake jolted Balochistan's Khuzdar district. The Pakistan Meteorological Department stated that the quake occurred at 10:07 am at a depth of twenty-five kilometres, with its epicentre located forty-eight kilometres northwest of Khuzdar at latitude 28.02 degrees north and longitude 66.20 degrees east. The department released this data through its Islamabad office. Earlier in August, an earthquake of magnitude 4.8 had jolted parts of Balochistan, including Zhob and Chaman, recorded at a depth of fifteen kilometres with its epicentre one hundred twenty-two kilometres northeast of Zhob. Those previous tremors caused fear and panic among residents, leading many people to come out of their homes while reciting verses from the Holy Quran. No loss of life or property damage had been reported from the earthquake.
