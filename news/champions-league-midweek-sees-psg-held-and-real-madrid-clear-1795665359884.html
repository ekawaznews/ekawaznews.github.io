Paris Saint Germain Denied by Roma at the Parc des Princes

According to Dawn, Paris Saint Germain lost their perfect record in the competition after AS Roma held them to a 2-2 draw. Al Jazeera added that the holders were an injury-hit squad struggling to maintain their momentum against a determined opponent. The match took place on a bitterly cold and wet night at the French capital stadium.

Luis Enrique managed the twice defending champions through a challenging fixture that tested their tactical flexibility. A important intervention came from Donyell Malen, whose goal denied the hosts all three points in front of the home supporters. Dawn specified that this marked the seventeenth goal in sixteen matches this season for the Dutch forward, with his strike arriving in the seventy eighth minute.

The outcome halted the winning run of the French side and allowed other contenders to close the gap in the standings.
