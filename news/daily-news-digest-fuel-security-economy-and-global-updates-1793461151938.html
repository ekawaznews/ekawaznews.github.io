Fuel Prices Shift as Diesel Rates Rise While Petrol Remains Stable

Petroleum product pricing authorities in Pakistan released official rate adjustments on Saturday. Ministry of Energy and OGRA notifications confirmed that high speed diesel prices increased. Meanwhile, authorities kept petrol pricing at its previous level across the country. This measure aims to balance global market conditions with domestic economic needs while maintaining continuous fuel supplies.

The announcement arrived while the public faces ongoing economic pressure. Geo News reported that the government implemented the fuel price revisions immediately nationwide. Observers note that these pricing adjustments directly impact transport costs and household budgets across different sectors.

Mansehra Assistant Commissioner Injured in Nowshera Attack

An official vehicle carrying Mansehra Assistant Commissioner Adil Waseem came under armed fire on Saturday. The incident took place in the Panah Kot area within Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province. Unidentified gunmen targeted the vehicle while the civil officer travelled back from his native village of Saleh Khana situated in the same district.

Emergency services and local police units responded urgently following the attack. Dawn reported that hospital officials confirmed the injured official is currently in stable condition. Law enforcement authorities launched a search operation in the surrounding region to locate the suspects responsible for the shooting.

Syria Considers Joining the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement

Damascus is actively evaluating whether to sign the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement. The official confirmed that discussions are underway regarding potential membership in the security framework.

This defense pact currently links Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkiye. Government representatives designed the initiative to transform the region into a prominent export oriented industrial center. The plan aims to reshape the economic foundations of the province.
