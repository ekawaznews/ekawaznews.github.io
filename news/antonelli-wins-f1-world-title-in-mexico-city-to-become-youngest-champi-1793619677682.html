Historic Milestone at Mexico City Grand Prix

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli made history on Sunday by securing the Formula 1 drivers' world championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix, becoming the youngest champion in the sport's existence. The Italian completed the contest in third place behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen, who took victory, and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, who crossed the line in second. The result provided Antonelli with an unassailable lead of 119 points in the overall standings, confirming his title victory with several races remaining in the 2026 season.

At 20 years and 68 days old, Antonelli broke the record previously held by German driver Sebastian Vettel. Reports from the BBC indicated that Antonelli surpassed Vettel's benchmark by three years and 66 days. In addition to setting an age record, he also became the first Italian driver to claim the drivers' crown since Alberto Ascari achieved the feat in 1953. Dawn reported that Antonelli wept with joy over his team radio as he took the chequered flag at the high-altitude Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez circuit.

Upon finishing the race, Antonelli struggled to speak over the radio due to intense emotion before expressing his gratitude to his team, according to coverage from the BBC. Race engineer Peter Bonnington congratulated the young driver over the radio, hailing his achievement after a remarkable debut championship campaign. Antonelli had entered the weekend needing only a top-nine finish if his team-mate won, or a top-ten finish if Hamilton took victory, to guarantee his mathematical triumph regardless of rival results.

A High-Stakes Race Interrupted by Red Flags

The grand prix was defined by intense track action, multiple collisions, and a major red-flag stoppage. Hamilton started the race from pole position, but immediate drama unfolded at the start as drivers fought for position into the opening corners. Verstappen capitalised on early clashes among front-runners to take his third win in four races. Antonelli, meanwhile, maintained a steady fourth position during his opening stint behind Leclerc, Verstappen, and Hamilton before moving forward as pit stops began.

A major interruption occurred on lap 45 when Valtteri Bottas suffered a front suspension failure under heavy braking for the first turn, causing his Cadillac entry to crash into the barriers. Safety officials initially deployed the safety car before bringing out the red flag to allow marshals to perform extensive barrier repairs. Dawn noted that the stoppage lasted for an agonizing 33 minutes, leaving 25 of the scheduled 71 laps to be completed in a high-pressure sprint to the finish once racing resumed.

When the race restarted, Leclerc suffered a heavy lock-up on his left front tyre, which allowed Antonelli to complete a pass on the following lap. A second safety car period was triggered with 12 laps remaining following a collision between Oscar Piastri of McLaren and Arvid Lindblad of Racing Bulls, which caused Lindblad to crash. When the final seven-lap sprint commenced, Verstappen led Hamilton and Antonelli in a tight train, with less than two seconds separating all three cars as they crossed the finish line.

Internal Tension Escalates Within Team Ferrari

While Antonelli celebrated his world championship, significant friction emerged within the Ferrari camp following early on-track disputes between Hamilton and Charles Leclerc. Hamilton had started from pole position, but Leclerc launched an aggressive challenge from third place through the opening sequence of turns. At the turn four and five chicane, Leclerc executed a pass on the outside, cutting across the grass before returning to the track in the lead of the race.

The maneuver forced Hamilton off the track while defending his line, allowing Verstappen to pass Hamilton into turn six and secure second place. Both Verstappen and Hamilton immediately voiced complaints over the team radio, requesting that Leclerc yield the lead. Race stewards later investigated the incident and handed Leclerc a five-second time penalty. Stewards noted that mitigation was applied because Hamilton had also left the track surface during the defensive maneuver.

The post-race reaction highlighted unresolved issues between the Ferrari team-mates. BBC Sport reported that Hamilton expressed frustration following the race, believing that early co-operation could have allowed Ferrari to secure a one-two finish. Leclerc later struggled with a severe flat spot on his tyre following his restart lock-up, but managed to defend fifth place against 2025 world champion Lando Norris. George Russell took sixth place for Mercedes despite serving a 10-second penalty for spinning Red Bull driver Isack Hadjar on the opening lap.

Antonelli's Path to Record-Breaking Championship

Antonelli's rise to the top of motor racing has been swift. BBC News reported that his father described him as a natural driver from the age of three, describing him as a boy who was essentially raised in a garage environment. Having turned 20 in late August, the young driver demonstrated remarkable consistency throughout his maiden championship campaign, mastering complex technical regulations and high-pressure racing environments across global circuits.

Throughout the Mexico City event, Antonelli focused on managing risk while keeping pace with the leaders. Mercedes had lengthened his final stint by bringing him in for fresh tyres just one lap before the safety car was called for Bottas' crash. That tactical timing allowed Antonelli to restart the race in a strong position, avoiding the incidents that plagued other competitors in the chaotic second half of the Grand Prix.

His championship victory marks a historic moment for Italian motorsport, ending a drought that stretched back over seven decades. Observers noted that Antonelli managed to remain calm during the final laps as Hamilton mounted an unsuccessful challenge against Verstappen for the race win. The Italian driver maintained his position at the tail of the leading trio, securing the podium placement required to put the championship title entirely beyond the reach of his competitors.

Looking Ahead to the Remaining Season

With the drivers' world championship now officially decided in Mexico City, the Formula 1 season enters its final stretch without the tension of a title decider. However, significant battles remain unresolved in the constructors' standings and individual driver rankings. Teams will quickly turn their focus to the next event as the current triple-header schedule continues without interruption across the Americas.

The championship moves next to Brazil for the Sao Paulo Grand Prix at the Interlagos circuit, scheduled to take place from 6 to 8 November. The upcoming race weekend will present fresh challenges as teams adjust to different atmospheric and track conditions following the high-altitude environment of Mexico City. Attention will also center on whether internal discussions within Ferrari can resolve the tactical differences displayed between Hamilton and Leclerc.

Motorsport analysts will be watching to see how Antonelli approaches the final races of his maiden championship-winning season now that the title is secured. With no championship pressure remaining, both Mercedes and Red Bull are expected to test development strategies for future campaigns. Fans and broadcasters around the world, including in Pakistan, will closely follow the closing rounds to see how the remaining positions in the championship table are finalized.
