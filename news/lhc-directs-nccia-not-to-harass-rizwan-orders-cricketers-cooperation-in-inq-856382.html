LHC Directs NCCIA Not to Harass Rizwan, Orders Cricketer's Cooperation in Inquiry

By Umer Javed | 21 September 2026 | 5 min read
Photo: Pragyan Bezbaruah / Pexels

Lahore High Court Chief Justice Aalia Neelum has restrained the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) from harassing cricketer Mohammad Rizwan during an ongoing inquiry. The court has also instructed Rizwan to fully cooperate with the investigation proceedings.

Lahore High Court Chief Justice Aalia Neelum has issued directives preventing the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) from causing any “harassment” to prominent cricketer Mohammad Rizwan in connection with an inquiry initiated against him. The development came to light on Monday, following a significant court hearing.

Concurrently, the Chief Justice has explicitly instructed the wicketkeeper-batsman to actively join and cooperate fully in the inquiry proceedings. This dual directive aims to ensure that while the investigation proceeds, the rights and dignity of the individual under scrutiny are also protected.

These instructions were formally articulated in a written court order pertaining to a hearing held on September 17. The hearing addressed a plea filed by Rizwan, challenging the NCCIA inquiry and a call-up notice he had received from the agency.

During the September 17 proceedings, Justice Neelum had presided over the matter. The court order later confirmed that Rizwan’s petition, which sought to challenge the inquiry and the notice, had been dismissed. Despite the dismissal of the petition, the court deemed it necessary to issue specific directives to both parties involved.

The Lahore High Court's Role in Judicial Oversight

The Lahore High Court stands as a key institution within Pakistan's judicial system, exercising original and appellate jurisdiction over a wide array of legal matters. Its mandate extends to safeguarding the fundamental rights of citizens, ensuring due process, and upholding the rule of law across its territorial jurisdiction, which primarily encompasses the province of Punjab.

In cases involving state agencies and individual citizens, the High Court frequently intervenes to balance the investigative powers of law enforcement with the constitutional protections afforded to every individual. This includes ensuring that inquiries are conducted fairly, without undue pressure or any form of harassment that could impede justice or violate personal liberties.

The Chief Justice, as the head of the High Court, plays a important role in setting precedents and issuing directives that shape legal practice and administrative conduct. Such orders serve as a reminder to all state institutions of their obligation to operate within the confines of the law and respect the rights of citizens, even when conducting sensitive investigations.

The court's decision to dismiss Rizwan's petition against the inquiry indicates that the court found the initiation of the inquiry by the NCCIA to be within its legal bounds. However, by simultaneously issuing instructions against harassment, the court has emphasized the manner in which such inquiries must be conducted, underscoring the judiciary's commitment to fair procedure.

Understanding the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA)

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) is a specialized unit within Pakistan's law enforcement framework, tasked with combating cybercrime across the country. Established to address the growing challenges posed by digital offenses, the agency plays a critical role in maintaining cybersecurity and ensuring the integrity of online spaces.

The NCCIA's mandate includes investigating a broad spectrum of cybercrimes, ranging from financial fraud and data theft to online harassment, defamation, and other illicit activities conducted through digital platforms. Its operations are governed by relevant cybercrime laws, providing it with the authority to initiate inquiries, summon individuals, and collect evidence in cases where digital wrongdoing is suspected.

The agency's work has become increasingly important in an era marked by rapid technological advancement and widespread internet usage. As more aspects of daily life, commerce, and communication shift online, the potential for cyber-related offenses has also risen, making the NCCIA's role indispensable in protecting citizens and national digital infrastructure.

While the NCCIA possesses significant powers to investigate and prosecute cyber offenses, its actions, like those of any state agency, are subject to judicial review and oversight. This ensures that the agency's investigative methods comply with legal standards and do not infringe upon the fundamental rights of citizens, a principle reaffirmed by the Lahore High Court's recent order.

Mohammad Rizwan: A National Figure

Mohammad Rizwan is a highly celebrated figure in Pakistani cricket, renowned for his exceptional skills as a wicketkeeper-batsman. He has consistently represented Pakistan in all formats of the game, earning widespread acclaim for his performances on both domestic and international stages.

Known for his gritty batting, excellent glovework, and leadership qualities, Rizwan has become a vital component of the national team. His contributions have been instrumental in numerous victories, solidifying his status as one of the most respected and admired cricketers in the country. His public image is generally one of dedication, professionalism, and sportsmanship.

As a prominent public figure, Rizwan's involvement in any legal matter naturally garners significant public and media attention. This makes the clarity and fairness of any inquiry involving him particularly important, not only for the individual but also for public trust in the justice system.

The fact that a high-profile athlete is subject to an inquiry by a cybercrime agency highlights that no individual, regardless of their public standing, is above the law. However, it also emphasizes the need for due process and protection against harassment, principles which the Lahore High Court has reiterated.

Balancing Rights and Responsibilities

The Lahore High Court's order represents a careful balancing act between the state's legitimate power to investigate alleged wrongdoing and an individual's right to be treated fairly and without harassment during such investigations. This balance is a cornerstone of any just legal system.
