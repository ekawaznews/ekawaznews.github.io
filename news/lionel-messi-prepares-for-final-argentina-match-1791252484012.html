The end of an era for Argentine football

Today marks a significant moment in the history of international football as Lionel Messi prepares to make his final appearance for the Argentina national team. According to reports from Dawn, the thirty-nine-year-old superstar will step onto the pitch for the last time in the Albiceleste jersey. This farewell friendly match takes place at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires. The event signals the conclusion of a career that has spanned more than two decades of service to his country.

The atmosphere in the capital is expected to be intense as eighty-five thousand spectators gather to witness the departure of a player who has defined a generation. Samaa TV noted that the match against Benin serves as the final send-off for the athlete. This event is not merely a game but a symbolic transition for the South American champions.
