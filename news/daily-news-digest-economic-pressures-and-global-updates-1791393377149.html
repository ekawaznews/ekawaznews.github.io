Pakistan Medical And Dental Council Postpones National Exam

The regulatory body governing medical professionals in the country recently announced a change in the schedule for the first part of the National Registration Examination. This assessment is intended for medical graduates who completed their studies abroad and wish to practice domestically. While the test was originally slated to take place on October 11, the governing body has moved the date to November 8, according to an official notification released on October 5.

This shift represents a significant change for candidates preparing to enter the professional medical field within the country. The council attributed the postponement to circumstances that could not be avoided amid the ongoing political situation. The examination serves as a standardized measure to ensure that all practitioners meet the required professional competencies before treating patients.

Candidates affected by this adjustment face a rescheduled timeline for their professional accreditation processes. Medical education monitors indicate that such disruptions can create logistical challenges for applicants who traveled from afar to take the assessment. Institutions involved in medical training are expected to provide updated guidance to students as the new test date approaches.
