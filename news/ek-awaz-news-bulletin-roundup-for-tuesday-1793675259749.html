National Grid Energizes Dhabeji Transmission Line

National Grid-Pakistan has successfully energized a new two hundred and twenty kilovolt transmission line extending from Jhampir-II to the Dhabeji substation, according to ARY News. This infrastructure development is designed to deliver a dependable electricity supply directly to the Dhabeji Special Economic Zone. Officials hope that consistent power will keep industrial facilities operational, encourage business investment, and generate employment opportunities across the region. Local authorities have emphasized that steady electrical currents are fundamental to running heavy machinery and maintaining uninterrupted commercial activity in manufacturing hubs.

Completed at a cost of one billion eight hundred and six point ninety million rupees, the project aligns directly with federal objectives to establish the zone as a major hub for industrial activity, as reported by ARY News. The reliable energy supply is expected to strengthen local supply chains and contribute significantly to the broader national economy. For young job seekers in Sindh, this initiative creates pathways to build sustainable careers without needing to relocate far from their homes. Industrial planners note that steady infrastructure lowers operational hurdles for incoming businesses.

MD National Grid, Engr. Altaf Hussain Malik, commended the engineering team for finishing the assignment successfully, noting that it represents a vital milestone for regional infrastructure. National Grid-Pakistan has stated its ongoing dedication to expanding transmission networks so that the advantages of economic progress reach communities throughout the country. Technical specialists point out that maintaining strong power corridors is essential for long term regional productivity and industrial viability.

Pakistan and Bangladesh Discuss Economic Cooperation

A high-level delegation from Bangladesh recently travelled to Islamabad to hold formal discussions with Pakistan's Finance Minister, Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb, regarding bilateral economic ties and trade expansion, ARY News reported. The visiting delegation was headed by Rashid Al Mahmud Titumir, Adviser to Bangladesh's Prime Minister on Finance and Planning. The meeting took place inside the premises of Pakistan's Finance Division. Both delegations used the platform to evaluate shared economic trajectories and discuss mechanisms for mutual growth.

During their talks, both delegations addressed the broader global economic climate, the distinct challenges confronting developing nations, and the collective necessity to preserve fiscal stability while pursuing inclusive growth. Muhammad Aurangzeb outlined Pakistan's ongoing structural reforms, tighter fiscal management strategies, and initiatives aimed at drawing private investment. He emphasized the necessity of disciplined debt management and financial sector development to secure long-term economic health and sustainable revenue generation.

In response, Rashid Al Mahmud Titumir shared Bangladesh's primary development goals, which center on investment promotion, industrial expansion, and job creation. Both sides reviewed opportunities for enhanced trade, manufacturing partnerships, and textile cooperation, noting the value of strengthening ties between their respective private sectors. The Finance Minister expressed readiness to share institutional expertise regarding public financial management and debt strategies, paving the way for closer bilateral engagement.

BBC News has reported that the Collins English Dictionary has selected the slang term mogging as its word of the year for twenty twenty-six. The expression is used informally to describe situations where someone or something outshines, outperforms, or upstages another person or object. Lexicographers noted a significant surge in usage across online platforms and everyday conversations among younger generations. Observers of language evolution track these shifts to understand how internet culture alters daily vocabulary.

Dictionary representatives explained that usage patterns are monitored to capture evolving language trends, observing that younger speakers frequently adopt and adapt terms to create humorous new slang. Although the word has roots in body-building communities and online forums dating back to the mid-twenty-tens, it has now entered mainstream vocabulary through digital communication channels.
