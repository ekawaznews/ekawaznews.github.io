Foreign Office Responds to Kabul Accusations

Official representatives in Islamabad issued a formal response addressing statements made by authorities across the western border. The Foreign Office firmly dismissed assertions concerning non combatant casualties resulting from ongoing security operations. According to coverage published by Dawn, officials stated that the national military approach consistently demonstrates restraint during security missions. This response came after statements emerged from the neighbouring country regarding military actions conducted by security forces.

The diplomatic response highlighted that any security measures undertaken remain strictly proportional to existing threats. Authorities stressed that operations target specific military and militant infrastructure rather than individuals uninvolved in armed actions. Such TV noted that the government rebuffed the allegations as completely unfounded. The Foreign Office issued a public statement clarifying the nature of these defensive measures to the domestic audience and international observers.

Details Regarding the Afghan Statements

The controversy emerged following statements from an official representing the administration in Kabul. According to reports from Dawn, a spokesperson for the Afghan Ministry of Foreign Affairs alleged during a media interview that over one thousand non combatants suffered casualties due to military actions conducted by Pakistan since a specific timeframe. These numbers were mentioned during a televised appearance that gained attention across regional media platforms.

The government maintains that its security personnel exercise caution during all operations. Official statements indicate that military responses are calibrated to match the intensity of threats faced by security forces.
