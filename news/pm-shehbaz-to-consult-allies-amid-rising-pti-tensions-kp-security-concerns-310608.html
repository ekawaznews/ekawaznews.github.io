PM Shehbaz to Consult Allies Amid Rising PTI Tensions, KP Security Concerns

By Umer Javed | 1 October 2026 | 5 min read
Photo: Habel Panggalo / Pexels

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif plans extensive consultations with coalition partners to address mounting political and security challenges, particularly in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. These discussions will cover issues in the PTI-ruled province and the threat of multiple planned protests.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif plans to hold extensive consultations with his allies to discuss the political and security situation, particularly in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, as political challenges mount for the PML-N government in light of multiple planned protests. These high-level meetings are intended to formulate a unified strategy to address the complex issues facing the federal government and its coalition partners.

The Prime Minister’s initiative comes at a time of heightened political activity and increasing tensions between the ruling coalition and the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI), which governs Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The discussions are expected to focus on provincial matters, the security, and the potential impact of various planned demonstrations across the country.

Sources indicate that these consultations will involve key leaders from allied parties, including the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP), a significant partner in the federal government. The objective is to achieve consensus on a way forward, addressing both immediate concerns and long-term stability.

Allies Engage on Key Issues

Recent engagements between the Pakistan Peoples Party and the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N) have already laid some groundwork for these broader discussions. Senior PPP leaders, including Senator Sherry Rehman and Qamar Zaman Kaira, have met with PML-N leaders, according to reports. Such meetings are a common feature of coalition politics, aimed at coordinating policy and strategy.

These preliminary discussions likely touched upon the issues that will now be formally addressed by the Prime Minister with all allied parties. The focus remains on strengthening the coalition's response to the challenges emanating from the political opposition and the security environment.

The PPP's participation in these discussions is significant, as its support is essential for the stability of the current federal setup. Ensuring all coalition partners are on the same page regarding critical national issues is a priority for the government.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Under Scrutiny

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, ruled by the archrival PTI, remains a central point of concern for the federal government. The province's political and security situation has been a recurring theme in national discourse, given its strategic location and the historical challenges it faces.

The Prime Minister's emphasis on KP in his consultations highlights the federal government's view of the province as a critical area requiring concerted attention. The dynamic between the federal government, led by PML-N, and the provincial government, led by PTI, often shapes the broader political narrative in the country.

The planned protests, reportedly organised by opposition elements, add another layer of complexity to the situation in KP and other parts of the country. The government's strategy will likely involve measures to maintain law and order while addressing the underlying grievances that might fuel such demonstrations.

Emergency Consideration in KP

Amidst these growing concerns, the possibility of declaring an 'emergency' in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa due to 'terrorism' is reportedly under consideration. Tariq Fazal Chaudhry stated this, indicating the seriousness with which the federal government views the security situation in the province.

The declaration of an emergency is a significant constitutional step in Pakistan, typically invoked under extreme circumstances such as war, internal disturbance, or a breakdown of the constitutional machinery in a province. Such a move would have far-reaching implications for provincial autonomy and federal-provincial relations.

While the exact nature of the 'emergency' being considered was not detailed, any such measure would be subject to strict constitutional provisions and political deliberation. It reflects the escalating concerns regarding the security environment and governance in the province.

Call for Dialogue and Constitutional Remedies

In response to the mounting tensions, senior political figures have called for dialogue and adherence to constitutional provisions. Nayyar Bokhari urged talks, emphasizing that the Constitution provides remedies if a government fails to fulfil its responsibilities.

Bokhari's statement highlights the importance of political engagement and finding solutions within the established legal framework. Dialogue is often seen as a preferred mechanism to de-escalate political crises and foster understanding between opposing factions.

The Constitution of Pakistan indeed outlines various mechanisms and remedies for situations where a provincial government might be perceived as failing in its duties. These provisions are designed to ensure the smooth functioning of the state while upholding the principles of federalism.

Such remedies could range from interventions to ensure financial stability, address law and order, or, in extreme cases, the imposition of Governor's Rule or other forms of federal oversight, all requiring specific constitutional procedures and parliamentary approval.

dealing with the Current Political

The PML-N led government is currently operating within a dynamic and often challenging political. Balancing the demands of coalition partners, addressing economic pressures, and managing opposition movements are persistent tasks.

The planned consultations with allies are the government's recognition of the need for a unified front. It aims to present a cohesive strategy to the public and the opposition, demonstrating strength and resolve in the face of adversity.

The outcome of these meetings will likely shape the government's approach to the upcoming protests and its overall strategy for engaging with the PTI-led provincial government in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The focus will remain on maintaining stability and ensuring effective governance.

The coming weeks will be critical as the Prime Minister and his allies deliberate on these significant challenges. The decisions made will have a direct bearing on Pakistan's political stability and security situation, particularly in the volatile region of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
