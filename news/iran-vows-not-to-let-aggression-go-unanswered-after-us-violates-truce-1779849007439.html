Iran vows not to let aggression go unanswered after US violates truce

U
Umer Javed
Staff Reporter
| 🕒 27 May 2026 at 07:30 am | 📖 4 min read
Share: 💬 WhatsApp 📘 Facebook ✈️ Telegram 🐦 Twitter

Opening section

Iran accused the United States of breaching their ceasefire on Tuesday and warned it was ready to retaliate, stating it "will not leave any evil unanswered" after overnight US strikes targeting Iranian missile sites and mine-laying boats threatened a deal to end the war.

The confrontation escalated dramatically, pushing global oil prices higher and intensifying calls for de-escalation from international powers.

Washington confirmed its military actions, specifying that the strikes were aimed at Iranian missile sites and mine-laying boats. This operation marks a significant departure from the recent period of relative calm.

Tehran, however, presented a different account of the overnight events. Iranian authorities stated that a US drone was downed and an F-35 aircraft was fired at during the hostilities. They also reported that a tanker suffered damage from an external explosion off the coast of Oman, adding another layer of complexity to the volatile situation.

The Brent benchmark oil price reacted sharply to the news, jumping by more than four per cent after US Central Command announced the new wave of bombings. This surge reflects growing market anxiety over potential disruptions to oil supplies from the critical Middle Eastern region.

Background and Context

The latest exchange of hostilities comes amid a fragile truce that aimed to de-escalate tensions between the two long-standing adversaries. This ceasefire was a temporary arrangement designed to pave the way for broader negotiations, or at least prevent direct military conflict from spiraling out of control.

The region has been a hotbed of geopolitical tension for decades, with the Strait of Hormuz, a vital shipping lane, frequently becoming a flashpoint. Any disruption in this area has immediate and far-reaching consequences for global energy markets and international trade.

The current "deal to end the war" mentioned by Iran refers to the delicate diplomatic efforts to stabilize the situation and prevent a wider conflict. The overnight strikes, however, severely jeopardize these efforts, bringing the two nations closer to an open confrontation.

Impact on Pakistan

The renewed escalation between Iran and the United States carries serious implications for Pakistan and its citizens. As a neighbouring country with significant strategic ties to the Middle East, Pakistan's economy and security are directly affected by regional instability.

The sharp rise in Brent crude oil prices will undoubtedly lead to increased costs for petroleum products within Pakistan. This will translate into higher inflation, affecting the prices of essential goods and services, and placing additional burden on the average Pakistani household.

also, regional instability poses a direct threat to Pakistan's security. Any widespread conflict in the Middle East could lead to an influx of refugees, disrupt trade routes, and potentially draw other regional actors into the fray, creating a complex security challenge for Islamabad.

Pakistan's government will face increased diplomatic pressure to balance its relationships with both Washington and Tehran. Maintaining neutrality while advocating for peace and stability will be a delicate act, requiring careful consideration of its foreign policy objectives.

Official Response

Following the US strikes, a prominent voice from Washington, Senator Marco Rubio, commented on the ongoing situation. Rubio stated that the Iran deal may take "a few days," insisting that the Strait of Hormuz will reopen "one way or the other." His remarks suggest a firm stance from certain US political circles regarding maritime access.

Internationally, China urged both sides to respect the truce and to resolve their dispute peacefully. Beijing's call for restraint highlights the global concern over the potential for a full-blown conflict that could have devastating consequences for the world economy and regional stability.

Iran's Supreme National Security Council spokesperson confirmed the nation's readiness to respond to any perceived aggression. "We will not leave any evil unanswered," the spokesperson stated, reinforcing Tehran's resolve to protect its interests and sovereignty.

What to Watch Next

The immediate focus now shifts to Washington, where President Trump is scheduled to hold a Camp David cabinet meeting to discuss the Iran crisis. This meeting is expected to determine the next steps in the US strategy and could signal a further hardening or softening of its position.

The fate of the existing truce, already severely strained, hangs in the balance. Observers will be closely watching for any diplomatic overtures or further military actions from either side. The international community's efforts to mediate and de-escalate the situation will also be under intense scrutiny.

The coming days are expected to be critical in determining whether the region descends into deeper conflict or if a pathway to renewed dialogue can be found. The stakes are incredibly high for all involved, with the potential for widespread implications across the globe.
