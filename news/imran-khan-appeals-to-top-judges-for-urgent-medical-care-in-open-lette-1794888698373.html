Imran Khan Appeals to Top Judges for Urgent Medical Care in Open Letter

Open Letter Sent to Top Judicial Officials

The incarcerated founder of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf and former prime minister, Imran Khan, has formally directed an open appeal to the highest levels of the judiciary in Pakistan, requesting immediate access to medical evaluation and treatment for himself and his wife, Bushra Bibi. The formal correspondence was addressed directly to the chief justices of three primary judicial bodies in the country, namely the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, and the Islamabad High Court. According to Dawn, the document outlines severe concerns regarding the physical health of both individuals who are currently under detention.

The transmission of this appeal was made public through the social media platform X by Barrister Salman Safdar, who serves as the legal representative for the former prime minister. Dawn reported that the legal counsel engaged in direct discussions with his client inside Adiala jail during two separate visits conducted on November 2 and November 11. It was during the second meeting on November 11 that the incarcerated politician instructed his lawyer to convey his specific concerns directly to the heads of the judiciary.

BBC News reported that the former prime minister has been held in custody since August 2023. Throughout his period of confinement, the ex-premier has maintained that he has been denied justice, making this recent legal step an extension of his ongoing efforts to seek judicial intervention regarding his treatment and conditions of confinement.

By selecting an open letter format addressed directly to the leadership of the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, and the Islamabad High Court, the defense team has ensured that the grievances regarding healthcare access are placed on public record. This step highlights the gravity of the situation as perceived by the former prime minister while held at Adiala jail.

Details of the Transmission and Legal Representation

The document published online carries the formal signature of Barrister Salman Safdar, confirming that the communication was prepared and delivered strictly under the explicit instructions of his client. According to Dawn, the lawyer noted that he was acting purely as a conduit to transmit the exact sentiments and requests of the former head of government.

During the meeting held on November 11 inside Adiala jail, the former prime minister specifically directed his legal counsel to "carry his words to your lordships". This directive formed the foundation of the letter submitted to the chief justices, establishing a formal line of communication between the prisoner and the heads of the judicial system.

The lawyer clarified that the contents of the document reflect the direct statements of the incarcerated leader. The document establishes that the former premier and Bushra Bibi require medical attention due to their current physical state, urging the top courts to step in and facilitate access to proper medical evaluations.

In professional legal practice, transmitting an open letter to multiple high courts represents a deliberate effort to seek official notice from judicial leaders. By securing signed confirmation from the legal counsel, the defense ensures that the authenticity of the message is established beyond dispute for both the court records and public awareness.

Concerns Over Personal Health and Custodial Care

Among the primary elements of the written appeal is an explicit warning regarding the life and health of the former leader. As reported by BBC News and Dawn, the former prime minister expressed serious anxiety over his survival in detention, stating that he fears he "may not return alive" if his health continues to worsen without appropriate care.

The letter details a noticeable deterioration in the health conditions of both the former prime minister and Bushra Bibi. While detailed clinical diagnostic files were not attached to the public summary, the letter characterizes the situation as urgent, requiring immediate access to qualified medical practitioners and facilities.

The responsibility for maintaining the health and safety of incarcerated individuals rests with the prison administration and state authorities. When serious allegations of deteriorating health emerge from high-profile prisoners held at facilities like Adiala jail, the judiciary often serves as the forum to review whether standard administrative protocols and basic rights are being maintained.

The statement that the former leader fears he "may not return alive" elevates the urgency of the request from a standard administrative petition to a significant human rights appeal. This expression of concern reflects the defense team's assertion that continuous incarceration without adequate medical access presents a direct threat to the well-being of both detainees.

The Role of Judicial Oversight and Jurisdiction

The appeal targets the top leadership of three distinct legal authorities: the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, and the Islamabad High Court. Addressing all three jurisdictions simultaneously indicates an effort to engage every potential legal mechanism capable of reviewing custody conditions and directing medical access.

In Pakistan, the higher judiciary retains authority over matters concerning fundamental rights and the treatment of prisoners within correctional institutions. When prisoners allege a denial of basic healthcare or claim that they have been denied justice, as BBC News noted in the case of the former prime minister, the courts may choose to evaluate the claims to ensure legal guidelines are observed.

Judicial authorities presented with such petitions have several options available under the law. Judges may call for official health reports from prison administration, order independent medical boards to examine the individuals, or issue specific instructions to ensure adequate facilities are made available to the prisoners.

The involvement of top judicial leaders provides a venue where concerns regarding prisoner treatment can be addressed outside the standard administrative hierarchy of the prison system. How these courts choose to handle this appeal could dictate the immediate care provided to both the former prime minister and Bushra Bibi at Adiala jail.

Context of Detention and Broader Political Setting

The former prime minister has remained in custody since August 2023, experiencing a prolonged period of detention that has remained central to national news coverage. The ongoing legal challenges facing the former leader have kept his health and confinement conditions under continuous examination by legal experts and the public alike.

According to BBC News, the former leader maintains that he has been denied justice during his time in detention. This perspective forms the backdrop of his open letter, framing the demand for medical care not merely as an isolated health request, but as part of a broader struggle regarding fair treatment under the law.

Questions regarding the health and treatment of prominent political figures in custody frequently draw attention from civil society and legal organizations. The request for medical intervention for both the former premier and Bushra Bibi highlights the ongoing friction between legal obligations for detainee welfare and the administration of penal facilities.

Any official action taken by the courts in response to this letter could influence public perception regarding the fairness of the judicial process. Observers will be noting whether the legal system grants the requested medical access or leaves the matter to standard administrative prison procedures.

Potential Outcomes and Elements to Monitor

In the coming days, attention will focus on whether the chief justices of the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, or the Islamabad High Court issue any formal notices or orders based on the letter provided by Barrister Salman Safdar. Judicial orders could mandate immediate medical examinations by independent doctors.

Another key factor to watch is the response of prison authorities at Adiala jail and relevant government departments. If the courts demand formal reports on the health status of the former prime minister and Bushra Bibi, prison officials will be required to submit documented evaluations to the court.

The legal team representing the former prime minister may also choose to follow up this open letter with direct procedural filings in court to reinforce the urgency of the medical plea. Continued legal representation by Barrister Safdar will likely play a central role in advancing these requests through official judicial channels.

The situation remains ongoing as all parties await official indications from the judicial branch. The outcome of this open letter will serve as a key indicator of how Pakistan's legal institutions manage high-profile appeals concerning prisoner health and claims of systemic injustice.
