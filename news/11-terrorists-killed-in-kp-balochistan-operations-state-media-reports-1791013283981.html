11 Terrorists Killed in KP, Balochistan Operations, State Media Reports

U
Umer Javed
Political Reporter
| 🕒 3 October 2026 at 12:41 pm | 📖 7 min read
Share: 💬 WhatsApp 📘 Facebook ✈️ Telegram 🐦 Twitter
📷 Photo: Marek Piwnicki / Pexels | ekawaznews.github.io

Security forces successfully killed 11 terrorists during intelligence-based operations (IBOs) conducted in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and Balochistan, state media reported on Friday. This development highlights the continuous commitment of Pakistan's security apparatus to eradicate militancy from the country's strategic regions.

State-run Radio Pakistan, citing security sources, confirmed that five individuals identified as Fitna al-Khawarij terrorists were eliminated during an IBO in South Waziristan district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The operation in South Waziristan is part of a broader, sustained campaign against militant elements seeking to destabilize the region.

The report from Radio Pakistan further elaborated that security forces are maintaining effective operations against Fitna al-Khawarij across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. These efforts are being conducted under the comprehensive national vision of Azm-i-Istehkam, which aims to consolidate peace and stability throughout the country.

The term Fitna al-Khawarij is officially used by the state to refer to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP). This designation highlights the government's perception of the group as an internal threat, drawing parallels to historical extremist ideologies.

The operations in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, particularly in areas like South Waziristan, hold significant importance due to their historical association with militant strongholds. The region, bordering Afghanistan, has long been a focal point for various extremist groups, including the TTP, which has sought to challenge state authority and disrupt peace.

South Waziristan, part of the erstwhile Federally Administered Tribal Areas (FATA), has witnessed numerous security operations over the past two decades. The integration of FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa was a landmark administrative decision aimed at bringing these areas into the national mainstream and extending state writ more effectively, thereby enhancing security and development initiatives.

The TTP, designated as a terrorist organization by Pakistan and several other countries, has been responsible for numerous acts of terrorism within Pakistan, targeting both civilians and security personnel. Its activities have caused widespread loss of life and significant damage to infrastructure, necessitating sustained counter-terrorism measures by the state.

The Azm-i-Istehkam vision, mentioned in the state media report, represents a renewed and holistic approach by Pakistan to combat terrorism and extremism. It encompasses not only military operations but also socio-economic development, judicial reforms, and public awareness campaigns, reflecting a multi-pronged strategy to achieve lasting peace and stability.

While the report specifically detailed the killing of five Fitna al-Khawarij terrorists in South Waziristan, the total count of 11 terrorists killed indicates that additional operations were conducted in other parts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. The precise affiliations of the remaining terrorists killed in these other operations were not specified in the initial state media reports.

Balochistan, Pakistan's largest province by area, faces its own set of complex security challenges. The province is strategically important due to its rich natural resources and its role in regional connectivity projects, including the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC). Militant groups, including separatist elements and religiously motivated extremists, have historically targeted security forces and infrastructure projects in the province.

Operations in Balochistan are therefore critical for safeguarding national assets and ensuring the safety of personnel involved in development projects. The presence of various non-state actors in the province necessitates a vigilant and proactive security posture from law enforcement agencies.

The commitment to securing Balochistan is part of Pakistan's broader national security policy, which prioritizes stability in all its provinces. The security forces conduct intelligence-based operations regularly to dismantle militant networks, interdict cross-border movements of terrorists, and maintain law and order across the vast and often rugged terrain of the province.

Pakistan has a long history of confronting terrorism and extremism, having launched several major military operations in the past. Operations like Zarb-e-Azb and Radd-ul-Fasaad achieved significant success in dislodging militants from their strongholds and reducing the overall threat level. However, the resilient and adaptive nature of these groups means that ongoing vigilance and proactive measures remain essential.

The current operations under the Azm-i-Istehkam vision build upon these past successes, aiming to consolidate gains and address the residual threat posed by various militant factions. This involves continuous intelligence gathering, targeted strikes, and efforts to cut off financial and logistical support for terrorist organizations.

The sustained pressure on militant groups in both Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan reflects the determination of the Pakistani state to ensure the safety and security of its citizens. The security forces, including the army, paramilitary forces, and police, operate in coordination to counter threats across the diverse geographical of the country.

These recent successful operations serve as a stark reminder of the sacrifices being made by Pakistan's security personnel in the line of duty. The fight against terrorism is a national endeavor, requiring continuous effort and comprehensive strategies to eliminate the root causes of extremism and maintain peace.

The government has repeatedly affirmed its resolve to root out terrorism in all its forms and manifestations. Statements from official channels consistently reiterate that no compromise will be made on national security and that those who seek to destabilize the country will be met with full force.

The intelligence-based nature of these operations is key to their effectiveness. By relying on precise intelligence, security forces are able to target specific individuals and networks, minimizing collateral damage and maximizing the impact on militant infrastructure. This approach is a cornerstone of modern counter-terrorism strategies.

The public's support for these operations is also a significant factor in their success. Communities in affected areas often cooperate with security forces, providing vital information that aids in the identification and apprehension of militants. This civil-military collaboration is essential for building a secure environment.

As Pakistan continues its efforts to foster economic growth and regional connectivity, the eradication of terrorism remains a top priority. The stability achieved through successful security operations provides a conducive environment for development initiatives to flourish, ultimately benefiting the populace and strengthening the nation.

Pakistan's Enduring Fight Against Militancy

Pakistan has been at the forefront of the global fight against terrorism for decades, facing significant internal and external challenges. The emergence of groups like the TTP, with their extremist ideologies, has necessitated a continuous and evolving counter-terrorism strategy. The operations reported by state media are a direct manifestation of this ongoing struggle.

The geographic proximity of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to Afghanistan presents unique security dilemmas. The porous border and the fluid situation in Afghanistan often have direct implications for Pakistan's security. Cross-border movement of militants and the availability of safe havens continue to be persistent concerns that require diplomatic and operational solutions.

The concept of Azm-i-Istehkam, or 'Resolve for Stability', is not merely a military campaign but a broader national vision. It aims to address the ideological, social, and economic dimensions of extremism alongside kinetic operations. This comprehensive approach recognizes that lasting peace requires more than just neutralizing immediate threats; it demands societal resilience and development.

For instance, efforts to rehabilitate areas affected by militancy, promote education, and create economic opportunities are considered integral parts of the Azm-i-Istehkam framework. These initiatives aim to prevent radicalization and integrate marginalized communities into the national fabric, thereby reducing the appeal of extremist narratives.

The security forces' ability to conduct coordinated operations across different provinces, as evidenced by the reported killings in both KP and Balochistan, highlights their operational reach and intelligence capabilities. This coordination is vital for disrupting militant networks that often operate across administrative boundaries.

The state's consistent use of terms like 'Fitna al-Khawarij' to describe groups like the TTP is also a deliberate strategy to delegitimize their ideology. By associating them with historical figures deemed heretical in Islamic tradition, the government seeks to counter their religious narrative and isolate them from mainstream society.

The challenges in Balochistan are distinct, involving a mix of ethno-nationalist insurgencies and religiously motivated extremism. The province's vast, sparsely populated areas can provide cover for militant activities, making intelligence gathering and interdiction operations particularly demanding for security forces.

Despite these challenges, Pakistan remains steadfast in its resolve to secure its borders and internal territories. The commitment to eradicate terrorism is a non-negotiable aspect of national policy, driven by the to protect civilian lives and ensure the country's economic progress.

The ongoing operations are the sacrifices made by countless personnel from the armed forces and law enforcement agencies. Their dedication and bravery are central to the nation's ability to withstand and overcome the persistent threats posed by terrorist organizations. The nation continues to mourn its fallen heroes while reaffirming its resolve to achieve a terrorism-free Pakistan.
