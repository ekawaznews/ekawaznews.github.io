Pakistan Missed Asian Games Women's Cricket Final After Sri Lanka's 83-Run Victory

By Umer Javed | 20 September 2026 | 3 min read
Photo: Ricky Febrianto / Pexels

Pakistan lost the semi-final of the Asian Games women's cricket tournament to Sri Lanka. Chamari Athapaththu's unbeaten 83 helped the hosts win by eight wickets.

Pakistan were eliminated from the Asian Games women’s cricket competition after a 178‑run chase by Sri Lanka, which finished with an eight‑wicket win at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan.

Match Setting

The semi‑final took place on Sunday in a venue that has hosted several international fixtures. Both teams entered the match with high hopes, as the Asian Games represent a major platform for women’s cricket in the region.

Sri Lanka’s Chase

Chasing a target of 178, Sri Lanka reached the score with thirteen balls to spare. The run chase was steady and disciplined, with the batting side taking calculated risks when necessary.

Chamari Athapaththu’s Dominance

Captain Chamari Athapaththu remained unbeaten on 83 from 50 balls, striking seven fours and six sixes. Her innings provided the backbone of the chase, guiding Sri Lanka to a comfortable finish.

Supporting Performance

Harshitha Samarawickrama contributed an unbeaten 17, helping to stabilize the innings after early wickets fell. Her presence at the crease added depth to the batting lineup.

Pakistan’s Innings

Pakistan posted 177 for 4 in their 20 overs, setting a target that seemed within reach for Sri Lanka. The Pakistani bowlers applied pressure but could not prevent the chase from slipping away.

Ayesha Zafar’s Contribution

Pakistan’s top scorer, Ayesha Zafar, made 71 off 40 balls, a rapid innings that anchored the chase. Her performance was a highlight in an otherwise mixed outing.

Asian Games Context

The Asian Games, held every four years, include cricket as one of the sports for women. The tournament offers a chance for emerging teams to compete on a continental stage.

Sri Lanka’s Path to the Semi

Earlier in the tournament, Sri Lanka had shown resilience in group matches, securing key victories that placed them in the semi‑finals. Their batting depth and disciplined bowling were evident throughout the competition.

Pakistan’s Campaign

Pakistan’s journey began with a strong showing in the group stage, earning victories against several opponents. The semi‑final loss marks the end of their quest for a medal in this edition of the Games.

Significance of the Final

The final match will decide the gold medalists in women’s cricket at the Asian Games. Both teams had the opportunity to showcase their skills on an international platform.

Reactions to the Result

Pakistan officials expressed disappointment over the loss, citing the need to strengthen the team for future tournaments. Sri Lankan players celebrated the win, recognizing the effort required to chase a challenging target.

Future Outlook

Both squads will now focus on upcoming international fixtures, with Pakistan looking to rebuild and Sri Lanka preparing to defend their position in the final. The experience gained in this match will be valuable for both teams.

Closing Thoughts

The semi‑final showcased the competitive spirit of women’s cricket in Asia. While Pakistan’s campaign ended in disappointment, the match added another chapter to the growing history of the sport in the region.
