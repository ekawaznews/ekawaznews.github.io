Border Clash and Official Statements at Angoor Adda

According to Dawn, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting strongly dismissed a statement issued by the Afghan defence ministry. This dispute follows an incident where Afghan security forces fired unprovoked on Pakistan Army border posts and residential neighborhoods in Lower South Waziristan on a Tuesday. The cross border attack violated established protocols and resulted in the tragic death of one local individual while leaving five others injured. Security forces responded swiftly to the firing.

later, the Afghan defence ministry alleged that Pakistani military personnel launched drones without provocation targeting the Angoor Adda bazaar and the commissioner office located in Paktika province. They claimed that Afghan forces launched retaliatory strikes against a nearby Pakistani military base and outposts, painting Pakistan as the aggressor. The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting termed these assertions a fabrication of lies. Meanwhile, security sources confirmed that a shell fired by the Afghan Taliban regime struck a residential home in Angoor Adda, killing one civilian and injuring five members of the same family, drawing deep concern over the threat posed to local residents by cross border shelling.

These developments highlight ongoing tensions along the border region. Observers note that such escalations could impact diplomatic ties between the two neighbouring administrations. The situation requires continuous observation to see if further border incidents occur.

Counter Terrorism Operations in Balochistan

According to ARY News, citing the Inter Service Intelligence, security forces eliminated nine terrorists belonging to fitna al hindustan during two intelligence based operations conducted in the Kachhi and Wadh Districts of Balochistan. These operations were launched following reported intelligence regarding the presence of terrorists tied to an Indian proxy.

In the Kachhi district operation, security forces successfully busted a terrorist hideout after an intense exchange of gunfire, sending seven Indian sponsored terrorists to hell while recovering weapons and ammunition. A separate operation in Wadh district involved security forces tracking and intercepting terrorist movement, resulting in an intense fire exchange that led to the death of two more Indian sponsored terrorists and the recovery of further weapons and ammunition. Sanitization operations are currently underway to clear the area of any remaining threats. Authorities stated that the relentless counter terrorism campaign under the vision of Azm e Istehkam, approved by the Federal Apex Committee on the National Action Plan, will continue at full pace to eradicate foreign sponsored terrorism.

What to watch next includes updates on the ongoing sanitization sweeps across Balochistan and further announcements regarding the Azm e Istehkam framework.

Pakistan Squad Named for U-19 Asia Cup Title Defence

According to Dawn, defending champions Pakistan announced a fifteen member squad for the upcoming under nineteen Asia Cup tournament scheduled to take place in Oman from November fifth to fourteenth. Farhan Yousuf will captain the squad, which the Pakistan Cricket Board stated is scheduled to depart for Oman from Karachi on October twenty ninth.

Pakistan has been placed in Group A alongside India, Nepal, and the tournament hosts, the United Arab Emirates, in the eight team competition. The selected squad features wicket keeper batters Abdul Qadir and Hamza Zahoor alongside batters Usman Khan, Hayam Khan, Jahangir Bilal, and Huzaifa Ahsan. All rounders Ahmed Hussain and Hasnain Abbas Dar have also earned selection. The pace attack features Abdul Subhan, Mohammad Seyam, Ali Raza, and Rizwanullah, while Mohammad Huzaifa and Niqab Shafiq provide the frontline spin options. Out of the selected players, Ali Raza and Huzaifa Ahsan were noted as not having been part of the previous squad.

Fans will be watching the team preparations closely as the squad travels to Oman to defend their continental title against strong regional opponents.

Karachi Board Releases Ninth Grade Science Results

According to ARY News, the Board of Secondary Education Karachi announced the annual examination results for Class ninth Science Group regular and private students for the year two thousand and twenty six. Board chairman Professor Musharraf Ali Rajput offered congratulations to all the students who successfully passed all six subjects and wished them future academic success.

Detailed figures provided by Controller of Examinations Ahmed Khan Chhutto showed that a total of one hundred eighty thousand and fifty four students registered for the examinations. Out of these, one hundred seventy seven thousand nine hundred and twenty three students appeared, while two thousand one hundred and thirty one candidates remained absent. Professor Rajput emphasized that transparency remained a top priority for the board during result preparation. Students can view their detailed results on the official website of the Board of Secondary Education Karachi.

Future oversight may focus on academic performance trends across the city following these published figures.

Noreen Niazi Hospitalized Amid Continued Detention

According to Dawn, incarcerated Pakistan Tehreek e Insaf founder Imran Khan sister Noreen Niazi was transferred from Lahore Kot Lakhpat jail to the Punjab Institute of Cardiology due to high blood pressure, according to her lawyer. Noreen Niazi, along with her sisters Aleema Khan and Uzma Khan, has been detained since September twenty first under the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance of nineteen sixty on the orders of the Lahore deputy commissioner.

The detained sisters are currently serving the second term of a thirty day detention under the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, which received an extension on October seventh. Noreen Niazi counsel Rana Mudassar Umer stated that jail doctors recommended her transfer to the hospital, where she arrived at approximately seven thirty in the evening. Hospital sources indicated that medical staff were conducting various tests. Her lawyer claimed that no family member had been officially notified regarding her deteriorating health condition.

Observers will monitor medical updates from the hospital and any legal challenges regarding the ongoing detention orders.

Resignation of Justice Mohsin Akhtar Kayani

According to Dawn, Lahore High Court Justice Mohsin Akhtar Kayani submitted his resignation, citing personal reasons that included health concerns following a recent surgery and reasons of conscience. In his resignation letter addressed to the president of Pakistan, Justice Kayani stated that serving at the Islamabad High Court and Lahore High Court had been a privilege and mentioned that his years of judicial service remained among the most fulfilling of his life.

During his tenure, Justice Kayani noted that he had disposed of more than twenty three thousand cases across various categories while attempting to uphold the Constitution, protect fundamental rights, and administer justice without fear or favour. However, he expressed concern that changes introduced through the twenty sixth and twenty seventh constitutional amendments had compromised the institutional integrity and independence of the higher judiciary.

Legal analysts suggest this resignation may provoke further discussions within legal circles regarding institutional reforms and judicial independence.
