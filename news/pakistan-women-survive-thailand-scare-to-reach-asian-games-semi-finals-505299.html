Pakistan Women Survive Thailand Scare to Reach Asian Games Semi-Finals

By Umer Javed | 17 September 2026 | 7 min read
Photo: Ketut Subiyanto / Pexels

Pakistan's women's cricket team secured a hard-fought victory against Thailand in the Asian Games Women’s Cricket Competition quarter-final on Thursday, winning by three wickets at the Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan. The narrow win, achieved with only two balls to spare, highlighted the team's resilience under pressure and secured their place in the tournament's semi-finals.

Pakistan's women's cricket team secured a hard-fought victory against Thailand in the Asian Games Women’s Cricket Competition quarter-final on Thursday, winning by three wickets at the Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan. The match, affected by rain, saw Pakistan chase down a challenging target in reduced overs, setting the stage for their semi-final appearance.

The quarter-final clash was a tense affair from the outset. Following a rain delay, the match was reduced, and Pakistan, after winning the toss and opting to field, put Thailand in to bat. The Thai side, demonstrating commendable determination, managed to post a total of 91 runs in their designated 11 overs. This score, while not exceptionally high, presented a stern test for the Pakistani batters given the pressure of a knockout encounter and the shortened format.

The chase proved to be more demanding than anticipated for Fatima Sana’s side. Pakistan found themselves in a precarious position, losing seven wickets in the process of pursuing the target. The scoreboard pressure mounted as the innings progressed, with the Thai bowlers making regular breakthroughs and keeping their team's hopes alive until the very last moments of the game.

Amidst the growing tension, opener Shawal Zulfiqar provided a vital impetus at the start of the innings. Her brisk knock of 25 runs from just 14 deliveries ensured that Pakistan remained within striking distance of the target, absorbing some of the early pressure and setting a foundation. Her aggressive approach was essential in a shortened game where every ball and every run held significant weight.

As wickets tumbled around her, it was captain Fatima Sana who stepped up to guide her team across the finish line. Her composed innings of 21 runs off 13 balls proved decisive. Sana's ability to maintain her composure and find boundaries under immense pressure was her leadership and batting prowess. Her contribution was absolutely essential in dealing with the final overs of the chase.

The victory was ultimately secured with only two balls remaining, underscoring the intensity and closeness of the contest. The narrow margin of success, coupled with the loss of seven wickets, indicates that Pakistan was pushed to its limits by a spirited Thai outfit. Such a tight finish in a knockout match can be a significant learning experience for any team, highlighting both strengths and areas requiring refinement.

The Asian Games Cricket Stage

The inclusion of cricket in multi-sport events like the Asian Games provides a significant platform for the sport's development, particularly for women's cricket. It offers an opportunity for national teams to compete on a prestigious stage, gain international exposure, and inspire a new generation of cricketers. For Pakistan, a nation with a deep passion for cricket, participating in and excelling at the Asian Games holds considerable importance.

The Asian Games cricket tournament brings together teams from across the continent, showcasing diverse playing styles and competitive spirit. For many participating nations, it represents one of the highest honours to compete for their country. The atmosphere of a multi-sport event, distinct from standalone cricket tournaments, adds another layer of excitement and pressure for the athletes.

Pakistan's women's team has consistently been a strong contender in regional and international competitions. Their participation in the Asian Games highlights their commitment to maintaining a prominent position in women's cricket. The journey through the tournament is not merely about individual matches but about demonstrating collective strength and strategic acumen.

Impact of Weather on Play

Rain delays are an inherent challenge in outdoor sports like cricket, and their impact can dramatically alter the complexion of a match. In the quarter-final against Thailand, the rain reduced the game to 11 overs per side, transforming it into a high-stakes sprint. This format demands immediate adaptability, aggressive batting from the outset, and precise bowling to contain opposition scorers.

For the batting side, a shortened game means less time to settle in and build an innings gradually. Every batter must aim for quick runs, taking calculated risks to keep the scoring rate high. The loss of early wickets in such a scenario can be far more damaging than in a longer format, as there is less opportunity for recovery.

Conversely, for bowlers, the reduced overs mean fewer chances to find rhythm, but also a heightened sense of urgency to pick up wickets and restrict runs. Captains must make rapid decisions regarding field placements and bowling changes, often relying on their most economical or wicket-taking options to make an immediate impact. The mental fortitude required to perform effectively under these changing conditions is substantial.

Pakistan's Path to the Semi-Finals

Reaching the semi-finals of a major continental competition like the Asian Games is a significant achievement for the Pakistan women's team. It places them among the elite teams in the tournament and brings them one step closer to securing a medal for the nation. The quarter-final victory, despite its tight nature, demonstrates the team's ability to perform under pressure and secure results when it matters most.

The performance against Thailand, while ultimately successful, also offers valuable lessons. The loss of seven wickets during the chase suggests that the batting line-up could benefit from greater stability and partnership building, even in a fast-paced, shortened format. Identifying areas for improvement in both batting and bowling will be vital as they prepare for the heightened challenge of the semi-final.

Fatima Sana's leadership, both through her batting and her captaincy, will be instrumental in the upcoming matches. Her ability to inspire confidence and make sound tactical decisions will be tested as the team faces potentially stronger opponents in the semi-finals. The entire squad will need to rally together, drawing on their collective experience and individual talents to overcome the next hurdle.

The Growing Profile of Women's Cricket in Pakistan

Women's cricket in Pakistan has seen a steady rise in profile and professionalism over recent years. The Pakistan Cricket Board has invested in developing infrastructure, coaching, and opportunities for female athletes, leading to improved performances on the international stage. Events like the Asian Games provide a critical platform to showcase this progress and further popularise the sport among young girls and aspiring cricketers.

The success of the national team acts as a powerful source of inspiration. When Pakistani women cricketers compete and achieve success at international events, it sends a strong message about the potential and talent present within the country. It encourages greater participation at grassroots levels and helps to break down traditional barriers, fostering a more inclusive sporting culture.

The team's continued presence in major tournament knockouts reinforces their standing as a competitive force in women's cricket. This consistency is not only a source of national pride but also contributes to the global narrative of women's sports gaining greater recognition and respect. The focus now shifts to the semi-final, with the nation keenly watching and hoping for further success.

Looking Ahead to the Semi-Finals

With the quarter-final hurdle cleared, Pakistan's women's team now faces the daunting task of competing in the semi-finals. The intensity of competition escalates significantly at this stage, with every team vying for a place in the final and a chance at a gold medal. The semi-final will demand a comprehensive performance from all departments of the game: batting, bowling, and fielding.

The team will need to learn from the challenges faced against Thailand and implement strategies that minimize risks while maximizing scoring opportunities. A more disciplined batting effort, coupled with incisive bowling and sharp fielding, will be essential to overcome the next opponent. The pressure of a semi-final can be immense, requiring strong mental resolve from every player.

The Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan, has provided the setting for this significant victory. Adapting to the local conditions, including any lingering effects of the weather, will be important for sustained performance. The team's coaching staff will undoubtedly be working on fine-tuning strategies and preparing the players both physically and mentally for the upcoming high-stakes encounter.

The entire nation will be rooting for the Pakistan women's team as they embark on this next challenge. Their journey in the Asian Games has been marked by resilience and determination, and they will be eager to continue their winning momentum. The semi-final presents an opportunity to further etch their names in the country's sporting history and bring home a prestigious medal from the Asian Games.
