Police Arrest Main Suspect in Amritsar Murder of Meed Sindhu

Fatal Shooting on Loharka Road

According to BBC Urdu, a fatal attack on a digital personality has triggered an extensive criminal investigation across state boundaries in India. The incident took place on the third day of October in the year two thousand and twenty six along Loharka Road in the city of Amritsar. Gunfire was directed at the victim during the assault, causing severe physical trauma. Although emergency steps were taken to move the injured person to a local hospital for urgent clinical care, death occurred before medical staff could provide treatment.

Following the deadly event, police authorities in Amritsar initiated criminal proceedings at the Amritsar Cantonment police station. The initial case was formally registered under various provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. This registration established the formal statutory basis for investigators to reconstruct the sequence of events, collect forensic material from Loharka Road, and pursue those responsible for planning and executing the lethal strike in broad daylight.

For audiences across the border in Pakistan, developments in Indian Punjab often draw close attention due to shared cultural touchpoints, linguistic ties, and mutual interest in regional entertainment networks. Violent incidents involving creative professionals in neighboring commercial hubs frequently generate widespread discussion among observers who track regional law enforcement trends and safety conditions for public creators.

Cross-Border Coordination and the Digital Trap

As reported by BBC Urdu, law enforcement teams from Amritsar carried out a joint operation alongside the crime branch of the Gujarat police to locate the main fugitive. Investigators identified the alleged principal gunman as Parminder, who also operates under the alias Kaka. After the violent episode on Loharka Road, the suspect fled Punjab and sought shelter hundreds of miles away in Ahmedabad, a major city within Gujarat state.

Amritsar Police Commissioner Harmanbir Singh Gill addressed reporters during an official briefing to explain how technical tools enabled officers to uncover the location of the fleeing individual. Gill noted that detectives utilized a unique technical strategy based on a digital financial code. The fugitive had reached out from Gujarat to establish contact with his brother, Amandeep Singh, who remained in Punjab, seeking fresh funds to finance his escape.

Law enforcement officials had already taken Amandeep Singh into secret custody before the requested transaction could proceed. Working with gathered intelligence and electronic logs, detectives maintained operational control over the phone of the detained brother. Crucially, the fugitive hiding out in Ahmedabad had no knowledge that his relative was sitting inside a police interrogation room in Punjab, leaving the shooter open to an electronic trap.

Financial Trail and Overseas Handlers

According to BBC Urdu, investigators uncovered an organized money transfer network linked to the crime. Amritsar Police Commissioner Harmanbir Singh Gill revealed that individuals stationed outside India transferred sixteen thousand rupees directly into the local financial account of Amandeep Singh. This monetary sum was intended to support the logistics of the assault and facilitate the immediate getaway of those involved.

Out of that sixteen thousand rupees, eleven thousand rupees were designated to be forwarded to Parminder, also known as Kaka, to sustain his life on the run in Ahmedabad. The remaining five thousand rupees were assigned to an individual identified as Paramvir Singh, who stands accused of providing the firearm used in the lethal attack on Loharka Road.

Taking advantage of the pending transfer, police personnel used the seized mobile handset belonging to Amandeep Singh to transmit a payment quick response code to Parminder. The fugitive was told that scanning the code with a smartphone was necessary to collect the promised cash. The moment the suspect initiated the scan, detection networks identified the geographical source of the request in the Vadaj neighborhood of Ahmedabad, allowing Gujarat police officers to move in quickly and execute the arrest.

Custody of Accomplices and Verification Process

The operation eventually led to the apprehension of six suspects connected to the planning and execution of the attack. BBC Urdu reported that the group of detained individuals includes the primary shooter Parminder, his brother Amandeep Singh, suspected weapon providers, and persons who allegedly carried out reconnaissance before the target was ambushed. Police listed the arrested suspects as Balraj Singh also known as Bunty, Vikas Singh, Baljinder Singh also known as Lally, Amandeep Singh, and Paramvir Singh.

Amritsar police also announced that they have established the precise identity of a second alleged shooter who took part in the gunfire on Loharka Road. Official spokespersons stated that law enforcement personnel are actively pursuing leads to take this second suspect into custody in the near future.

To ensure total accuracy before confirming the arrest of the main shooter, detectives conducted an electronic verification session. Investigators initiated a video call connecting the officers in Gujarat with the inquiry team in Punjab. During this call, detectives examined the footwear worn by Parminder in Vadaj and matched the shoes directly to visual records captured by surveillance equipment during the shooting on Loharka Road.

Life and Public Profile of Meed Sindhu

The victim was recognized by hundreds of thousands of online followers under the professional title Meed Sindhu, though government documents and civil records listed the birth name as Madhusudan. Operating a dedicated beauty and makeup salon, the creator regularly distributed styling advice, cosmetic tutorials, and popular videos synchronized with traditional and contemporary Punjabi songs. The creator had built an audience exceeding two hundred thousand followers on the Instagram network and appeared as an actor in the Punjabi film Oye Bholle Oye.

Beyond cosmetic work, Sindhu spoke transparently across digital channels about personal identity and membership in the LGBTQ community. Past media interviews revealed that the stylist experienced prejudice, harsh criticism, and mockery throughout life, alongside expressions of warmth and acceptance from admirers and close acquaintances.

In an archival conversation, Sindhu reflected on developing a passion for cosmetics and fashion during early childhood years. Family dynamics played an important role in this journey; the creator recalled that a supportive mother offered consistent encouragement at home, even while neighborhood voices offered persistent taunts. Subsequent educational years in Delhi brought broader acceptance, eventually securing wider family approval for a career in the beauty industry.

The online personality also confronted homophobic trolling on social platforms over the years but refused to remain silent. During an appearance on a regional podcast program discussing sexual identity, Sindhu insisted that being part of the community is not a mental disorder and asserted personal autonomy by declaring an intention to remain authentic regardless of outside judgment.

Preceding Threats and Judicial Expectations

The fatal shooting followed earlier public warnings that circulated on social platforms. According to BBC Urdu, a recorded video had surfaced several months prior to the October attack in which an unidentified individual appeared to direct direct threats at Sindhu while demanding an in-person meeting. The presence of this prior warning has led observers to ask questions regarding how threats against public figures are monitored before violence occurs.

Legal analysts suggest that upcoming hearings in Punjab will place heavy emphasis on the forensic validity of the electronic steps taken by investigators. Defense attorneys and state prosecutors will likely examine how the payment code was deployed, how overseas funds reached domestic bank accounts, and whether the electronic chain of custody meets statutory standards under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.

In the coming weeks, attention will remain focused on whether law enforcement successfully tracks down the second identified gunman and uncovers the full identity of the overseas handlers who provided the financial resources. The trial process is expected to test both the physical testimony of witnesses from Loharka Road and the digital records gathered during the operation that spanned from Punjab to Gujarat.
