Police Book Three for Alleged Gang Rape of Azeri Woman in Lahore

By Umer Javed | 27 September 2026 | 5 min read
Photo: Nahmad Hassan / Pexels

Defence-C police in Lahore have registered a case against three unidentified men on charges of allegedly gang raping a 20-year-old Azeri woman. The woman, visiting Pakistan for business, filed an application detailing the incident that occurred after she met two women for dinner.

Defence-C police in Lahore on Saturday registered a case against three unidentified men on charges of subjecting a 20-year-old Azeri woman to alleged gang rape. The incident has prompted a swift police response, with investigations now underway to identify and apprehend the suspects involved in this serious crime.

The Azeri national was reportedly in Pakistan for business purposes and was residing in a house situated near Raiwind Road in Lahore. Her presence in the city was part of her professional engagements, making the alleged incident a matter of significant concern for authorities tasked with ensuring the safety of all individuals, including foreign visitors.

According to the application filed by the victim with the police, the sequence of events began when two women approached her with an invitation for dinner. She accepted the invitation, and the three women later went to a local café. It was at this café that they reportedly met three men.

The woman stated in her application that she could identify both the men and the two women by face. This detail is expected to be a significant lead for the investigating authorities as they work to piece together the events and identify those responsible.

Her account further detailed that after their meeting at the café, the men offered to drive her and the two other women. The offer led to a change in location, as she was then taken to an unidentified flat. She reported that alcohol was consumed at the flat, and she was also served.

The registration of a First Information Report (FIR) marks the official commencement of the legal process. Under Pakistani law, an FIR is a document prepared by the police when they receive information about the commission of a cognisable offence. It is the important first step that enables the police to formally investigate a crime, make arrests, and gather evidence.

Police Investigation Underway

Following the registration of the case, Defence-C police have initiated a thorough investigation. Police officials have stated their commitment to pursuing all available leads to ensure justice for the victim. The process will involve gathering forensic evidence, examining the crime scene, and interviewing potential witnesses to corroborate the victim's statement.

Investigators will likely focus on tracing the identities of the two women who initially approached the Azeri national, as well as the three men she met at the café. The victim's ability to identify the individuals by face provides a vital starting point for the police, who will likely use various investigative techniques, including photographic identification and database searches, to establish their identities.

Such investigations often require careful coordination and resource allocation, particularly when foreign nationals are involved. The police are expected to handle the case with sensitivity and professionalism, ensuring that the victim receives appropriate support throughout the legal process.

Legal Framework for Sexual Assault Cases in Pakistan

In Pakistan, crimes of sexual assault, including gang rape, are dealt with under strict provisions of the Pakistan Penal Code. These laws carry severe penalties for offenders, reflecting the gravity with which the state views such crimes. The legal framework is designed to provide justice to victims and deter potential perpetrators.

The legal process typically involves the collection of evidence, medical examinations, and the recording of witness statements, all of which contribute to building a strong case for prosecution. Once suspects are identified and arrested, they are presented before a court of law, where they undergo trial. The judiciary plays a critical role in upholding justice by ensuring fair proceedings and delivering verdicts based on evidence presented.

The government and law enforcement agencies have repeatedly affirmed their dedication to combating crimes against women and ensuring their safety. Special measures and helplines have been established in various regions to facilitate the reporting of such incidents and provide immediate assistance to victims.

Ensuring Safety for Foreign Visitors

Pakistan has been actively working to promote tourism and business investments, attracting a growing number of foreign visitors. The safety and security of these individuals are vital for the country's image and its efforts to foster international relations. Incidents involving foreign nationals are therefore treated with utmost seriousness by the authorities.

Authorities are committed to creating a safe and welcoming environment for all visitors. This commitment involves not only proactive security measures but also a swift and effective response to any reported incidents. The current investigation is this resolve, as the police move quickly to address the alleged crime.

Embassies and consulates often play a supportive role in such situations, providing assistance to their citizens who may become victims of crime while abroad. While no official statement from the Azeri embassy has been reported, it is common practice for diplomatic missions to monitor such cases and offer necessary support to their nationals.

Broader Societal Context and Call for Justice

The alleged incident highlights the persistent challenges societies face in ensuring the safety of women. It also highlights the responsibility of law enforcement to act decisively and transparently in bringing perpetrators to justice. Public trust in the justice system is reinforced when such cases are handled efficiently and fairly.

Ek Awaz News, like other responsible media outlets, believes in reporting facts objectively and highlighting the importance of due process. The focus remains on the ongoing investigation and the expectation that justice will be served. The community, along with legal and human rights organizations, often calls for expeditious trials and stringent punishments for those found guilty of sexual assault.

The incident also serves as a reminder for both local residents and visitors to exercise caution and remain vigilant in their interactions, particularly in unfamiliar surroundings. While the police continue their efforts to secure the city and maintain law and order, individual awareness and prudence remain important.

As the investigation progresses, further details are expected to emerge. The police have urged anyone with information related to the case to come forward and assist them in their efforts to identify and apprehend the suspects. The pursuit of justice for the Azeri woman remains the primary objective for the authorities involved in this sensitive case.
