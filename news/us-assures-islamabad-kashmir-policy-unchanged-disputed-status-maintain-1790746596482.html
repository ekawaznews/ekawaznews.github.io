US Assures Islamabad: Kashmir Policy Unchanged, Disputed Status Maintained

U
Umer Javed
Political Reporter
| 🕒 30 September 2026 at 10:36 am | 📖 6 min read
Share: 💬 WhatsApp 📘 Facebook ✈️ Telegram 🐦 Twitter
📷 Photo: Rahul Sapra / Pexels | ekawaznews.github.io

WASHINGTON: The United States has assured Pakistan that its position on the Kashmir dispute remains unchanged, confirming Washington continues to regard the region as a disputed territory requiring a settlement between Pakistan and India, according to diplomatic sources.

This significant assurance from Washington followed a period of diplomatic engagement initiated by Islamabad, which sought clarification regarding recent remarks made by a senior US official. The episode highlights the delicate nature of the Kashmir issue in international diplomacy and the consistent efforts by Pakistan to ensure the international community maintains a principled stance.

Origin of the Diplomatic Query

The need for this clarification arose after controversial remarks by US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor. Ambassador Gor described Jammu and Kashmir as an “important part of India” during a visit to Srinagar on August 19. Such a statement from a high-ranking US diplomat carries considerable weight and can be perceived as an indication of policy, prompting immediate concern in Islamabad.

Ambassador Gor’s comments were made in a sensitive context, given the longstanding dispute over the region. Pakistan has consistently maintained that Jammu and Kashmir is an internationally recognized disputed territory, a position supported by United Nations resolutions. Therefore, any statement that appears to deviate from this understanding elicits a strong diplomatic response from Pakistan.

Islamabad Seeks Clarification

According to Pakistani diplomatic sources, Islamabad promptly sought clarification from Washington regarding Ambassador Gor’s remarks. The objective was to ascertain whether the Ambassador’s statement signalled a shift in the long-held US policy on Kashmir. Such diplomatic channels are routinely used to address potential misunderstandings or perceived changes in policy from allied nations.

Pakistan’s diplomatic corps engaged with their US counterparts to convey its concerns and to seek a reaffirmation of the established US position. This proactive approach by Islamabad highlights the centrality of the Kashmir dispute to Pakistan’s foreign policy and its commitment to ensuring international adherence to the principles of self-determination and international law concerning the region.

Reaffirmation of US Stance

In response to Pakistan’s query, the United States conveyed that its position on the Kashmir dispute remains unchanged. This confirmation from Washington reaffirmed that the US continues to view the region as a disputed territory. The US stance, as communicated, emphasizes that the issue necessitates a settlement between Pakistan and India, reflecting a neutral position that does not endorse either country’s claim over the entirety of the region.

The assurance from Washington is seen as a significant diplomatic development, as it dispels any ambiguity that might have arisen from Ambassador Gor’s statement. It reaffirms the consistent US policy, which has historically refrained from taking a definitive side on the sovereignty of Kashmir, instead advocating for a peaceful resolution through dialogue between the two South Asian nuclear powers.

Historical Context of the Kashmir Dispute

The Kashmir dispute dates back to the partition of British India in 1947, when the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir became a point of contention between the newly formed nations of Pakistan and India. The issue has since led to several conflicts and remains a primary source of tension in the region.

The United Nations Security Council passed several resolutions in the late 1940s and early 1950s, calling for a plebiscite to determine the future of Jammu and Kashmir. These resolutions are foundational to Pakistan’s position, which advocates for the right of self-determination for the people of Kashmir. India, while initially agreeing to a plebiscite, later maintained that Kashmir's accession to India was final.

For decades, the international community, including major global powers like the United States, has largely recognized Kashmir as a disputed territory. This recognition is rooted in the historical context, the UN resolutions, and the unresolved nature of the conflict. Any deviation from this understanding by a major power is therefore met with considerable concern and diplomatic action.

The Significance of Diplomatic Language

In international relations, the precise wording used by diplomats and government officials holds immense significance, especially concerning sensitive geopolitical issues. A statement from an ambassador, even if interpreted as a personal opinion, can be seen as reflecting the official policy of their government.

Ambassador Gor's description of Jammu and Kashmir as an “important part of India” diverged from the careful diplomatic language typically employed by the US, which generally refers to the region as ‘Indian-administered Kashmir’ or ‘Pakistan-administered Kashmir’ without prejudice to the final resolution of the dispute. This careful nuance is designed to maintain impartiality and encourage dialogue.

The swift diplomatic response from Pakistan and the subsequent clarification from Washington highlight the importance of maintaining consistency in international diplomatic discourse on such a sensitive issue. It serves as a reminder that policy statements, even seemingly minor ones, are scrutinized for their implications on established positions and international norms.

Implications for Regional Stability

The Kashmir dispute has direct implications for peace and stability in South Asia. Both Pakistan and India are nuclear-armed states, and tensions over Kashmir have historically escalated to military confrontations. A consistent international stance that recognizes the disputed nature of the territory is seen by many as essential for encouraging a peaceful resolution.

Any perception of a shift in the policy of a major global power like the United States could potentially embolden one party or dishearten another, thereby complicating efforts towards de-escalation and dialogue. The reaffirmation of the US position, therefore, contributes to maintaining a degree of predictability and balance in the region's complex geopolitical.

Pakistan has consistently called for the international community to play a constructive role in facilitating a just and peaceful resolution to the Kashmir dispute, in accordance with the aspirations of the Kashmiri people and the relevant UN resolutions. The US assurance reinforces the notion that the international community, including Washington, still sees the dispute as requiring an bilateral settlement.

Looking Ahead

The diplomatic exchange highlights the ongoing vigilance required in international relations, particularly concerning long-standing disputes with significant humanitarian and geopolitical dimensions. Pakistan's diplomatic efforts in seeking clarification were successful in obtaining a reaffirmation of the US position, which aligns with the international understanding of Kashmir as a disputed territory.

This episode serves as a reminder of the enduring sensitivities surrounding the Kashmir dispute and the necessity for international actors to adhere to established diplomatic principles. The consistent application of international law and recognition of the disputed status of Kashmir remain central to any efforts aimed at fostering a lasting peace between Pakistan and India.
