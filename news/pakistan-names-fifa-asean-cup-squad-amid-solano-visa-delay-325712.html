Pakistan Names FIFA ASEAN Cup Squad Amid Solano Visa Delay

By Umer Javed | 20 September 2026 | 6 min read
Photo: GMB VISUALS / Pexels

Pakistan has announced a 23-member squad for the FIFA ASEAN Cup in Indonesia, with head coach Nolberto Solano expected to join the team directly in Indonesia following a visa delay. The Pakistan Football Federation confirmed the squad, which includes both local and international players.

Pakistan has officially announced its 23-member squad on Saturday for the upcoming FIFA ASEAN Cup, scheduled to take place in Indonesia. A significant development accompanying this announcement is that head coach Nolberto Solano will join the national team directly in Indonesia, as a visa delay prevented his participation in the preceding training camp held in Islamabad.

The Pakistan Football Federation (PFF), the governing body for football in the country, confirmed the final composition of the squad. This selection process has brought together a diverse group of athletes, including players who have been training within Pakistan and those members of the national team currently based abroad, reflecting the modern challenges and opportunities in assembling a competitive international side.

The absence of the head coach from the preparatory camp in Islamabad marks a procedural hurdle for the national team's preparations. Training camps are generally considered essential for a coach to observe players firsthand, implement tactical strategies, and foster team cohesion ahead of a major international competition. Solano's direct arrival in Indonesia means he will have limited time to integrate fully with the entire squad before the tournament commences.

According to reports from sources familiar with the situation, the delay in Solano obtaining his Pakistani visa stemmed from an initial application submitted under an incorrect category. Upon identification of this administrative error, the Pakistan Football Federation promptly resubmitted the application. However, the rectifying process later led to an unavoidable delay in its approval and issuance, preventing Solano from joining the team during its important pre-tournament phase in the capital.

The role of a head coach extends far beyond match days, encompassing the meticulous planning of training sessions, individual player development, and the cultivation of a cohesive team spirit. His absence from the Islamabad camp meant that assistant coaches were tasked with overseeing the squad's preparations, ensuring that training protocols and tactical groundwork continued without interruption.

International tournaments such as the FIFA ASEAN Cup provide invaluable exposure for Pakistani footballers, offering them a platform to compete against regional opponents and gain experience at a higher level. For a nation striving to elevate its standing in global football, consistent participation and competitive performances in such events are considered vital steps forward.

Squad Selection and Preparation Dynamics

The selection of the 23-member squad represents a culmination of efforts by the Pakistan Football Federation and the coaching staff to identify the most suitable talent available. The squad comprises a mix of experienced players and emerging talents, carefully chosen to balance defensive solidity, midfield creativity, and attacking prowess. The inclusion of players based abroad highlights the PFF's strategy to tap into a wider pool of talent, bringing in individuals who may benefit from exposure to different footballing environments and standards.

Assembling a national team often involves intricate logistical planning, particularly when players are scattered across various geographical locations. The PFF is responsible for coordinating travel, accommodation, and training facilities, ensuring that all selected players can converge and prepare effectively for international assignments. This logistical complexity is further compounded when unforeseen administrative issues, such as visa delays for key personnel, arise.

The training camp in Islamabad would have served as a critical period for the coaching staff to fine-tune strategies, assess player fitness, and build camaraderie among the diverse group of players. For those travelling from abroad, it would have also provided an opportunity to acclimatize to local conditions and integrate with their teammates from Pakistan.

While the assistant coaches have been diligently managing the team's preparations in Solano's absence, the direct presence of the head coach is often regarded as indispensable for final tactical adjustments and motivational leadership. His eventual arrival in Indonesia will necessitate a rapid integration process, requiring both the coach and the team to adapt quickly to the circumstances.

The Impact of Visa Delays on International Sports

Visa processing delays are not uncommon in international sports, but they can pose significant challenges for national federations and coaching staff. Such administrative hurdles can disrupt meticulously planned schedules and potentially impact team morale and preparation. For a developing footballing nation like Pakistan, where resources might be more constrained, these delays can present even greater operational difficulties.

The initial application under the wrong category suggests a procedural misstep that later required correction. While the PFF moved to rectify the error promptly, the nature of bureaucratic processes often means that such corrections can take time, leading to the current situation. This incident highlights the importance of meticulous attention to administrative details when dealing with international travel and personnel for sporting events.

Ensuring that all members of the coaching staff and playing squad possess the necessary travel documentation well in advance of international engagements is a constant challenge for sports federations worldwide. The PFF's experience with Solano's visa situation will likely inform future protocols to prevent similar occurrences, emphasizing the need for thorough checks and early applications.

The focus now shifts to how quickly Solano can integrate with the team upon his arrival in Indonesia and the extent to which the assistant coaches have managed to maintain the team's readiness. The players themselves will need to demonstrate resilience and adaptability, understanding that such challenges are sometimes part of the journey in international sports.

Aspirations for the FIFA ASEAN Cup

Participation in the FIFA ASEAN Cup is more than just a series of matches for Pakistan; it represents an opportunity to test the squad's capabilities on an international stage and to gauge the progress of football development within the country. Each tournament provides valuable lessons, whether through victories, draws, or defeats, contributing to the long-term growth of the sport.

The national team carries the hopes and expectations of football enthusiasts across Pakistan. Supporters will be keenly watching their performances, anticipating competitive displays and a demonstration of the hard work put in by the players and coaching staff. The tournament also serves as a platform for individual players to showcase their skills, potentially attracting attention from clubs regionally and internationally.

The Pakistan Football Federation continues its efforts to promote and develop football at all levels, from grassroots initiatives to the national team. International competitions are a critical component of this strategy, providing benchmarks for progress and inspiring younger generations to take up the sport. The ASEAN Cup is another step in this ongoing journey, irrespective of the challenges faced in preparation.

The squad, now confirmed and set to travel to Indonesia, will be looking to put the preparatory challenges behind them and focus entirely on their performances on the field. Their collective determination and spirit will be vital as they represent Pakistan in this international football event.
