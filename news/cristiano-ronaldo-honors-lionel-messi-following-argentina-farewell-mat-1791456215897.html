Farewell Appearance at the Monumental Stadium

According to Al Jazeera and Dawn, Lionel Messi brought an end to his international career in front of eighty-five thousand supporters at the Monumental Stadium in Buenos Aires. The friendly fixture saw Argentina claim a three to zero victory over Benin, with the thirty-nine-year-old playmaker scoring the closing goal of the evening and providing assists for the earlier two strikes. The match represented his two hundred and eighth appearance in the national jersey, drawing an emotional reaction from spectators inside the ground and crowds gathered outside the venue.

Dawn reported that tearful fans congregated outside the stadium to honour their captain ahead of the final whistle. Messi had guided the national side through several major campaigns, having captained the squad to the two thousand and twenty-two World Cup trophy alongside victories in the Copa America in two thousand and twenty-one and two thousand and twenty-four. He also helped Argentina secure the two thousand and twenty-two Finalissima. His retirement came after leading Argentina to the World Cup final in the United States in July, where the team finished as runners-up behind Spain.

Writing on social media the day following the match, Messi addressed his supporters directly. As quoted by Al Jazeera, he wrote: "Yesterday was my last game, and I already miss wearing the blue and white. I know I’m making the right decision." He reflected on his journey, calling it full of joy and sadness while noting that every step was worth taking. Messi also thanked his clubs for respecting his feelings and allowing him to join the national squad whenever required, while offering gratitude to his supporters and expressing how deeply he would miss them.

A Tribute Across Decades of Fierce Competition

Al Jazeera reported that Portuguese forward Cristiano Ronaldo was among the hundreds of thousands of people who responded to Messi's farewell message on Instagram. The two players shared a competitive rivalry that lasted for more than fifteen years, dominating global discussions and dividing football enthusiasts into passionate camps. During their careers, the pair accumulated thirteen Ballon d'Or awards between them, establishing themselves as the two prominent figures of their era.

Replying directly to the social media post, Ronaldo offered warm praise to his long-standing adversary. As reported by Al Jazeera, Ronaldo wrote: "Leo, after so many years of defending your country, you’ve made history that will last forever." The forty-one-year-old forward followed this statement by writing: "I have the utmost respect for what you’ve accomplished with Argentina," before closing his response with: "A hug."

Their rivalry reached its peak during nine years in Spain, where Messi played for Barcelona and Ronaldo represented Real Madrid. The frequent domestic and European encounters between the two clubs heightened the drama surrounding both individuals. Their final meeting on a football pitch took place in January two thousand and twenty-three during an exhibition match in Saudi Arabia. In that contest, Ronaldo featured for a Riyadh Season Team while Messi played for Paris Saint-Germain, with both men getting on the scoresheet in front of an enthusiastic crowd.

Words of Gratitude From Teammates and Peers

Tributes also arrived from fellow professionals who shared dressing rooms and pitches with Messi over the years. According to Dawn, Brazilian forward Neymar praised his former colleague on Instagram prior to the final game against Benin. Neymar had played alongside Messi at both Barcelona and Paris Saint-Germain, forming a close friendship that transcended national rivalries.

As reported by Dawn, Neymar wrote: "It’s not only Argentina that thanks you; the world thanks you for everything you have done for football." The Brazilian attacker added: "Thank you for making me love football even more. It was an honour for me to have played against you and even more so to make history alongside you." The message reflected the admiration felt by colleagues across South America and Europe who competed with and against the Argentine captain.

Dawn also reported that Rodrigo De Paul, Messi's close friend and Argentina teammate who plays alongside him at Inter Miami, showed deep emotion as the squad prepared for the match. Fellow players understood the weight of the occasion, recognizing that the fixture against Benin marked the departure of a leader who had stood at the center of the national team setup for over two hundred international matches.

Contrasting Moments for Two Football Legends

The timing of Messi's farewell coincided with a challenging period for Ronaldo on the international stage. According to Al Jazeera, the very day Messi took the field for his final match, Ronaldo issued an apology for his controversial walkout from the Portuguese national team camp. The incident followed a disagreement between Ronaldo and Portugal coach Jorge Jesus.

Al Jazeera reported that Ronaldo's apology left the door open for a possible return, though it remains uncertain whether the veteran forward will ever wear the Portugal shirt again. While Messi received a celebrated send-off on home turf, Ronaldo found himself dealing with internal squad friction and public debate regarding his international standing.

At club level, the two players continue their careers on different continents. Messi currently turns out as a playmaker for Inter Miami, whereas Ronaldo represents Saudi Pro League champions Al Nassr. Despite playing outside traditional European leagues, both athletes remain focal points of attention, with their choices continuing to draw scrutiny from international broadcasters and supporters worldwide.

Reverberations Among Football Followers in Pakistan

The farewell of the Argentine captain holds considerable meaning for football fans across Pakistan. While cricket remains the dominant sport in the country, international football tournaments and European leagues enjoy substantial viewership in urban centers such as Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Local supporters have spent years debating the merits of Messi and Ronaldo, organizing watch parties and following every El Clasico match during their time in Spain.

For many young Pakistani followers, the careers of Messi and Ronaldo represented their introduction to global football culture. Local fan clubs routinely shared footage of their goals, debated award selections, and tracked their international performances during World Cup tournaments. The end of Messi's international journey symbolizes the passing of an era that shaped sports viewing habits for an entire generation of local enthusiasts.

Sports observers in Pakistan note that the absence of Messi from future international tournaments could shift how local audiences engage with major competitions. Broadcasters that previously relied on the drawing power of the Argentine forward may find themselves highlighting emerging stars, while casual viewers might re-evaluate their interest in international fixtures without the presence of the number ten shirt.

Uncertain Horizons and What to Watch Next

Following his international retirement, observers will watch how Argentina adjusts to the departure of its long-serving leader. The national side must look toward future tournaments without the playmaker who captained them to World Cup and Copa America titles. The transition presents a test for the coaching staff and remaining squad members, who will need to share the creative burden previously carried by Messi.

At the same time, the football world awaits developments regarding Ronaldo's status with Portugal. Fans and analysts will track whether coach Jorge Jesus and the Portuguese football federation accept his apology and reintegrate him into the national squad, or whether his international career has quietly reached its conclusion. Any official statement from the Portuguese camp will carry significant weight in the coming months.

Meanwhile, both players will focus on their club commitments with Inter Miami and Al Nassr. Supporters across the globe, including in Pakistan, will monitor whether these two veterans can secure further silverware before concluding their playing days entirely. Although their direct rivalry on the international stage has now closed, the debate surrounding their achievements will likely continue for years to come.
