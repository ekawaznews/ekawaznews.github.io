National Resources Limited Identifies Copper and Gold Sites in Chagai

New Discoveries in Balochistan Mineral Sector

National Resources Limited has shared updated findings regarding mineral exploration activities in Balochistan following extensive field evaluations in the southwestern region of Pakistan. According to Dawn, the company announced on Friday that preliminary geological investigations have confirmed the presence of valuable metals across eight separate locations situated within Chagai district. The disclosure was made during a formal gathering focused on national mining potential, where corporate leaders presented technical outcomes to industry stakeholders and observers.

The operational campaign in Chagai represents a extended effort by corporate geologists to assess subterranean wealth through targeted technical programs. Mining projects in remote regions require significant upfront investment, preliminary mapping, and subsurface physical sampling before technical teams can verify underlying potential. The presentation of these initial findings provides the broader business community with structured updates on how exploration efforts are progressing across licensed territories in Balochistan.

Public gatherings such as the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 serve as standard platforms for corporate entities to share operational progress and strategic vision with government representatives, sector peers, and potential investors. According to Dawn, National Resources Limited Chairman Muhammad Ali Tabba selected this event to present recent outcomes from the company work program. Publicly sharing technical metrics permits industry analysts to assess how systematic exploratory campaigns are defining the natural resource characteristics of the district.

Technical Details of Drilling Results

The foundation of the current report rests upon an extensive subsurface testing initiative carried out across the target zone. Dawn reported that National Resources Limited completed nearly twenty five thousand metres of drilling in the area to collect physical core samples from deep underground. This high volume of subterranean drilling has provided technicians with sufficient continuous geological data to move toward formulating an initial official resource calculation.

Detailed analysis of core samples extracted from the subsurface revealed valuable technical information regarding mineral grade and continuity. According to Dawn, specific measurements from one individual drill hole identified a continuous mineralized section measuring two hundred ninety five metres in length. Chemical assays of this stretch showed average concentrations of zero point forty three per cent copper together with zero point thirteen grams of gold per metric tonne of material.

Further examination of the core data revealed even higher concentrations within specific sub-sections of that drill hole. Within the larger two hundred ninety five metre zone, technical teams identified a distinct internal segment measuring one hundred thirty two metres. Dawn noted that this specific segment recorded a significantly higher copper concentration reaching zero point eighty three per cent, demonstrating the presence of localized high-grade mineral deposits within the broader geological structure.

In mineral exploration, verifying consistent metal concentrations over continuous vertical and horizontal distances is a vital step toward understanding subsurface geometry. Extracting physical cores over thousands of metres allows geological teams to build three-dimensional models of underground structures. Collecting comprehensive sampling data remains a necessary technical phase before any corporate entity can determine the viability of long-term development plans.

Geographical Scope and License Parameters

The geographic boundary of the recent findings spans a sizable portion of local terrain within Chagai district. According to Dawn, the eight individual mineralized sites identified by National Resources Limited cover a combined footprint of five point six square kilometres. This designated acreage is situated in the Darigwan locality, where preliminary surface mapping and subsequent deep drilling revealed consistent geological indicators.

The location of the Darigwan site holds strategic context within the company overall exploration footprint in the area. According to Dawn, the newly mapped five point six square kilometre zone is directly adjacent to an earlier mineral discovery made by the same firm at the Tang Kaur copper and gold site. The geographic proximity of these two discovery areas suggests the potential existence of a continuous mineral belt running across local rock formations in the region.

Current operational activities are governed by formal regulatory permits issued for mineral exploration in the province. According to Dawn, work is currently proceeding under exploration licence EL214. Continued technical evaluations within this specific licensed block are structured to help company geologists determine the true spatial parameters, boundary limits, and total volumetric potential of the underground deposits.

Parallel Activities by Corporate Entities

Exploration work in Balochistan is not restricted to a single corporate entity, as multiple commercial organizations are actively examining the natural resource potential of southwestern Pakistan. The simultaneous execution of technical work by different operators reflects a broader corporate focus on evaluating subsurface assets across remote administrative divisions in the country.

Independent reports confirm that other major corporate players are conducting parallel exploration campaigns in the same administrative area. ARY News reported that Mari Energies announced a copper and gold discovery within Chagai district. This announcement highlights that distinct corporate organizations are independently reaching positive indicators as they assess subterranean structures across local acreage.

While Dawn provided detailed coverage of the technical figures presented by National Resources Limited, the report by ARY News demonstrates that multiple firms are contributing to ongoing geological research in the region. Concurrent updates from different corporate entities draw attention to the wider technical activity taking place across the district, where deep drilling and laboratory analysis are standard practice.

The accumulation of data from multiple corporate programs provides a broader context for evaluating the total natural wealth of the area. As different operators execute drill programs and publish preliminary technical findings, industry analysts gain a clearer picture of regional mineralization. The combined work of these enterprises helps build a comprehensive body of geological knowledge regarding southwestern Pakistan.

Potential Industry Impact and Future Steps

The conclusion of deep drilling operations marks a distinct transition point in the project lifecycle for National Resources Limited. Having gathered extensive core samples across thousands of metres of drilling, the enterprise is now shifting its operational focus from raw field data collection to analytical evaluation. According to Dawn, Chairman Muhammad Ali Tabba confirmed that the organization is actively advancing toward its first formal estimate of area mineral resources.

Formulating an initial resource estimate involves a rigorous technical process combining spatial modeling, statistical evaluation, and quality verification of assay results. Technicians and geologists process sample data to calculate the total estimated mass of mineralized rock and the average grade of contained metals. Establishing a standardized resource model is an essential preliminary step before any future economic, environmental, or engineering assessments can be undertaken.

If subsequent resource calculations confirm suitable deposit size and metal grade, such outcomes could influence broader economic planning for the regional mining sector. Observers note that establishing defined mineral reserves can attract further commercial interest, encourage infrastructure development in surrounding areas, and inform long-term corporate decision-making regarding potential capital investment in Balochistan.

What to Watch Next in Chagai District

In the coming period, industry observers and market participants will closely watch for the release of the initial formal resource estimate by National Resources Limited. The forthcoming calculation will establish official baseline figures regarding total tonnage and metal content within the five point six square kilometre Darigwan block, providing concrete metrics to evaluate project potential.

Geological teams will also continue technical field programs under exploration licence EL214 to refine subsurface interpretations. According to Dawn, additional exploration activities across the license area are expected to help establish the complete physical scale and structural continuity of the discovered deposits. Further infill drilling and geological modeling will help determine whether the Darigwan and Tang Kaur zones connect into a single contiguous system.

Finally, stakeholders will monitor ongoing developments from other commercial entities operating in the region, including Mari Energies following its announcement reported by ARY News. As multiple organizations complete analytical phases and present follow-up reports, the cumulative outcomes will clarify the long-term mineral prospectivity of Chagai district and Balochistan as a whole.
