Agricultural Impacts and Irrigation Challenges in Sindh

Dawn reported that the cultivation of Rabi crops, including wheat and oilseeds, has begun in lower Sindh amid a twenty two percent irrigation water shortage forecast by Irsa. Farmers in Sindh are increasingly turning to oilseed crops, particularly mustard, for quicker economic returns. Greater domestic production could help reduce Pakistan’s dependence on imported edible oil, which consumes substantial foreign exchange.

Dawn reported that Pakistan imported a record 3.48 million tonnes of palm oil worth 3.78 billion dollars in the financial year 2026, compared with 3.21 million tonnes costing 3.4 billion dollars in the financial year 2025. The average import price rose to 1,078 dollars per tonne from 1,056 dollars. Data from the Pakistan Oilseed Department and the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics shows that Pakistan’s edible oil import bill reached 5.86 billion dollars in 2025 for 4.52 million tonnes. This included 3.671 million tonnes of imported oil and 0.853 million tonnes extracted from 3.848 million tonnes of imported oilseeds, illustrating a clear shift in agricultural trends.
