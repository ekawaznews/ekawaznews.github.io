Police Probe Alleged Rape at Lovely Professional University

By Umer Javed | 30 September 2026 | 4 min read
Photo: KATRIN BOLOVTSOVA / Pexels

Police investigate claim that a female student was raped at Lovely Professional University in Punjab, sparking campus unrest and mass hostel evacuations. Investigation ongoing.

Police are investigating allegations that a female student was raped at Lovely Professional University (LPU) in Jalandhar, Punjab, following reports that the incident triggered widespread unrest and forced many students to leave their hostels.

Background of LPU

LPU, founded in 2005, is one of India’s largest private universities, hosting more than 20,000 students across engineering, management, law, and medical streams. The campus spans over 500 acres and boasts modern infrastructure, including residential hostels, sports complexes, and research facilities. The institution has been a popular choice for students from across the country and abroad, contributing to its diverse student body.

Details of the Allegation

According to reports, the alleged incident occurred early in the morning on campus, leading to a swift response from security staff and local police. The victim, a senior undergraduate, claimed that she was assaulted by an unidentified individual while walking to her hostel. The claim was immediately reported to the campus security office, which in turn notified the Jalandhar Police Department.

Police Response

Police officials confirmed that they have opened a formal case under Section 375 of the Indian Penal Code. They are currently verifying the claim and collecting evidence from the scene, including CCTV footage and forensic samples. The department has also requested the university to provide access to all relevant security logs and to cooperate fully with the investigation.

Campus Unrest and Hostel Evacuation

Following the allegation, students across multiple departments staged spontaneous sit‑ins and marches outside the university gates. The protests were largely peaceful, but the intensity of the demonstrations prompted the administration to temporarily shut down the hostel complex. Hundreds of students were moved to temporary accommodation on campus grounds to ensure their safety while the investigation proceeds.

University’s Stance

In a statement to reporters, LPU’s vice‑chancellor said that the institution is cooperating with law enforcement and has taken steps to support the affected student. He added that the university would conduct an internal review of its security protocols and would provide counseling services to all students affected by the incident.

Previous Security Incidents

LPU has faced several security challenges in the past, including a campus lockdown in 2019 following a threat of violence and a series of vandalism incidents in 2021. In response, the university upgraded its surveillance systems and increased the presence of security personnel on campus. The current situation has reignited concerns about the adequacy of these measures.

Local Police Involvement

The Jalandhar Police Station, which oversees law and order in the area, has assigned a dedicated team to the case. Officers are conducting interviews with witnesses and reviewing footage from the university’s extensive CCTV network. They have also reached out to neighboring institutions to gather any additional information that may aid the investigation.

Legal Framework and Potential Proceedings

Under Indian law, a rape case is treated as a grave offense, with the victim’s testimony and forensic evidence forming the core of prosecution. If the investigation finds sufficient evidence, the accused may face charges that carry a minimum sentence of ten years imprisonment, with the possibility of life imprisonment depending on the severity of the act and aggravating factors.

Student Union Reaction

Representatives from the LPU Student Union expressed deep concern over the incident and called for stricter enforcement of campus security. They urged the administration to conduct an independent inquiry and to keep students informed about the progress of the investigation. The union also announced a support helpline for students experiencing distress.

Expert Opinion

A legal expert who requested anonymity explained that the success of the case will largely depend on the integrity of evidence collection and the cooperation of the victim. He noted that delays in filing the police report can hamper the ability to gather important forensic data, which is often decisive in such cases.

Impact on International Perception

Incidents of sexual violence on university campuses can influence international student recruitment. While LPU has maintained a strong reputation for academic excellence, the current situation may prompt potential students and their families to reassess safety protocols. The university’s response to the crisis will therefore be closely watched by both domestic and international stakeholders.

Ongoing Investigation

The investigation remains active, with police scheduled to conduct additional forensic examinations and witness interviews over the coming days. The university has pledged to maintain transparency and will release further updates as the case develops. Students and staff are advised to follow official channels for accurate information.

Conclusion

The alleged rape at Lovely Professional University has triggered a swift response from both law enforcement and the university administration. While the investigation continues, the incident highlights the need for strong campus safety measures and transparent communication between institutions and authorities. All parties involved remain committed to ensuring justice and maintaining a secure environment for students.
