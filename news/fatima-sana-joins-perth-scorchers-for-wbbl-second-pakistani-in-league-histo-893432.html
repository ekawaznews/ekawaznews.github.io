Fatima Sana Joins Perth Scorchers for WBBL, Second Pakistani in League History

By Umer Javed | 3 October 2026 | 6 min read
Photo: Yogendra Singh / Pexels

Pakistan women’s cricket captain Fatima Sana has signed a one-year contract with Perth Scorchers for the upcoming Women’s Big Bash League season, the Australian club announced. This landmark move sees the 24-year-old all-rounder become only the second Pakistani player to feature in the prestigious WBBL.

Pakistan women’s cricket captain Fatima Sana has officially signed a one-year contract with the Perth Scorchers for the upcoming Women’s Big Bash League (WBBL) season, the Australian club confirmed on Saturday. This significant development marks a new chapter for the prodigious all-rounder and for Pakistani women’s cricket on the international stage.

The 24-year-old will bring her dynamic all-round capabilities to the Scorchers’ squad, a move that is expected to strengthen their campaign in WBBL 12. Her participation is initially confirmed for the first eight matches of the season, which is scheduled to commence on October 29. This initial commitment highlights the practical considerations involved when a top international player balances national duties with global league commitments.

Fatima Sana’s availability beyond these initial eight matches is contingent upon receiving special permission from the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). This clearance would allow her to miss a national training camp, which is slated to precede the Pakistan national side’s anticipated tour of the West Indies in December. The PCB’s decision will be closely watched, as it will determine the full extent of her involvement with the Scorchers and reflect the board's policy on player participation in foreign leagues.

A Landmark Moment for Pakistani Women's Cricket

This signing is particularly noteworthy as Fatima Sana becomes only the second Pakistani player in history to feature in the Women’s Big Bash League. Her inclusion in one of the world's premier women's T20 competitions is her individual talent and the increasing recognition of Pakistani women cricketers on the global circuit. It highlights the progress being made in developing female cricketing talent within the nation, providing a platform for players to gain invaluable experience alongside international stars.

The Women’s Big Bash League has established itself as a highly competitive and respected tournament since its inception. It regularly features the best cricketing talent from Australia and around the world, providing a high-octane environment that challenges players to perform at their peak. For Fatima Sana, joining the Perth Scorchers offers an unparalleled opportunity to hone her skills against elite competition, adapt to different playing conditions, and bring back a wealth of experience to the national team.

As captain of the Pakistan women’s team, Fatima Sana carries significant responsibility and is a leading figure in the sport domestically. Her leadership qualities, combined with her prowess as an all-rounder, make her a valuable asset for any team. The Scorchers will undoubtedly benefit from her ability to contribute with both bat and ball, providing important breakthroughs with her medium-fast bowling and adding impetus to the lower order batting.

Fatima Sana's Profile and Expected Impact

Fatima Sana’s career trajectory has been on a consistent upward trend. Known for her disciplined bowling and useful contributions with the bat, she has emerged as a vital component of the Pakistani national side. Her appointment as captain further solidified her standing as a key player and a leader on the field. Her experience in international cricket, including various T20 internationals, will be a significant advantage as she adapts to the demands of the WBBL.

The Perth Scorchers, a franchise with a strong history in the WBBL, will be looking to Fatima Sana to provide stability and match-winning performances. Her ability to bowl at different stages of an innings, from powerplay to death overs, coupled with her aggressive batting style, makes her a versatile player. Integrating an international captain into their squad is a strategic move, aiming to enhance the team’s overall performance and tactical depth.

The Scorchers’ management would have keenly observed her performances in international fixtures and domestic circuits before making this significant investment. Bringing in a player of her caliber from a strong cricketing nation like Pakistan aligns with the WBBL’s ethos of global participation and talent exchange. Her presence is not only beneficial for the team but also adds to the league’s diversity and appeal.

Balancing National Commitments and League Aspirations

The conditional nature of Fatima Sana’s contract, specifically regarding the potential extension, highlights a recurring challenge for international cricketers. The delicate balance between representing one's country and participating in lucrative and skill-enhancing global leagues often requires careful negotiation and approval from national boards. The Pakistan Cricket Board has a history of supporting its players in pursuing opportunities abroad, provided it does not conflict with national duties.

The anticipated tour of the West Indies in December is a significant fixture for the Pakistan women’s team. The training camp preceding this tour is essential for team cohesion and preparation. Therefore, the PCB’s decision on whether to grant Fatima Sana permission to miss this camp will weigh the benefits of her WBBL experience against the immediate needs of the national team’s readiness. Such decisions often reflect the board’s long-term vision for player development and international competitiveness.

Should permission be granted, it would not only allow Fatima Sana to complete a full WBBL season but also signal a progressive approach from the PCB, recognizing the value of exposure to top-tier league cricket. This exposure can be instrumental in a player's growth, allowing them to learn new techniques, understand different game strategies, and build resilience under pressure, all of which ultimately benefit the national squad.

The Broader Impact on Pakistani Cricket

Fatima Sana's participation in the WBBL serves as an inspiration for aspiring female cricketers across Pakistan. Her journey from local cricket to captaining the national team and now securing a contract in an elite international league showcases the potential pathways available. It sends a clear message that talent and hard work can open doors to global opportunities, motivating more young girls to pursue cricket seriously.

This move also strengthens the cricketing ties between Pakistan and Australia. Such player exchanges foster goodwill and facilitate the sharing of cricketing knowledge and expertise. For Pakistani cricket, having its captain perform in a league as prominent as the WBBL enhances the nation's reputation in women's cricket and draws international attention to the talent pool available within the country.

The experience gained by Fatima Sana will be invaluable not only for her personal development but also for the entire Pakistan women’s team. She will return with fresh perspectives, new skills, and an enhanced understanding of playing under diverse conditions and against varied opposition. This knowledge can be shared with her teammates, contributing to the overall improvement and strategic depth of the national squad as they prepare for future international assignments and tournaments.

As the WBBL 12 season approaches, all eyes will be on Fatima Sana as she steps onto the Australian grounds, representing not just the Perth Scorchers but also the aspirations of Pakistani women's cricket. Her performance will undoubtedly be followed with keen interest by fans back home, hopeful that her stint will pave the way for more Pakistani talent to shine on the global stage.
