Twin Cities Gear Up to Counter PTI’s September 27 Protest

By Umer Javed | 11 September 2026 | 5 min read
Photo: Andrew Patrick Photo / Pexels

The administrations of Rawalpindi and Islamabad have initiated extensive preparations to counter the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf's (PTI) announced countrywide protest on September 27. Measures include requests for activist detentions, container arrangements, and procurement of anti-riot equipment.

The administrations of Rawalpindi and Islamabad have commenced comprehensive preparations to counter the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf’s (PTI) announced countrywide protest, scheduled for September 27. These preemptive measures include a formal request for the detention of political activists, the strategic arrangement of shipping containers, the procurement of additional law enforcement personnel, and the acquisition of anti-riot gear.

Law enforcement agencies in Islamabad are actively gathering intelligence pertinent to the upcoming demonstration. This includes collecting detailed information regarding the political party’s preparations for the protest, as well as monitoring the activities of its key leaders, workers, and active participants. Such intelligence gathering is a standard procedure in managing large-scale public gatherings to ensure public order and safety.

Sources within the police department have confirmed that contractors responsible for providing and arranging containers for law enforcement operations have been formally alerted. There has also been a demand for a significant number of these containers, which are typically used to block roads, secure sensitive areas, and manage crowd flow during major protests or security situations in urban centers.

The proactive approach by the twin cities’ administrations reflects a broader strategy to maintain law and order during politically charged events. Historical precedents in Pakistan indicate that large-scale protests often lead to significant disruptions in daily life, including traffic congestion, closure of businesses, and heightened security alerts across the affected regions.

Political protests are a regular feature of Pakistan’s democratic, with various parties frequently resorting to public demonstrations to voice their grievances, exert political pressure, or rally public support. The right to peaceful assembly is enshrined in the country’s constitution, but it is often balanced against the state’s responsibility to maintain public order and protect citizens’ rights to free movement and safety.

The deployment of additional police personnel is a standard protocol for managing demonstrations of this scale. These personnel are tasked with crowd control, maintaining peace, and ensuring that the protest remains within legal boundaries. Training for anti-riot situations is also a continuous process for police forces, equipping them with the necessary skills and equipment to handle potential unrest.

Procurement of anti-riot gear, which typically includes shields, batons, tear gas, and protective clothing, is a precautionary measure. It enables law enforcement agencies to respond effectively to situations where protests might turn disorderly or violent, aiming to disperse crowds and restore order with minimal harm to individuals.

Requests for the detention of activists are usually made under various sections of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) Ordinance. This ordinance grants authorities the power to detain individuals deemed likely to act in a manner prejudicial to public safety or the maintenance of public order. Such detentions are often a contentious aspect of protest management, frequently drawing criticism from political parties and human rights organizations.

The MPO Ordinance has been a tool frequently employed by successive governments in Pakistan to preemptively manage political dissent. Its application often targets key organizers and mobilizers of protests, aiming to reduce the scale or intensity of planned demonstrations by removing influential figures from the scene. The legal validity and ethical implications of these detentions are often debated in courts and public forums.

The context of PTI’s announced protest is significant, given the party’s history of large-scale demonstrations across the country. In recent years, PTI has frequently mobilized its supporters for protests on various national issues, often leading to confrontations with law enforcement and significant disruption in major cities, particularly Islamabad and Rawalpindi, which serve as the political nerve center of the country.

The twin cities, due to their proximity to national institutions, diplomatic enclaves, and the seat of government, are particularly sensitive to political unrest. Administrations here are always on high alert for planned protests, and their strategies are often developed with a view to protecting critical infrastructure and ensuring the security of government operations.

Traffic management is another major component of these preparations. When major arteries are blocked by containers or large gatherings, city traffic comes to a standstill, affecting commuters, businesses, and emergency services. Contingency plans are therefore developed to reroute traffic and minimize the impact on essential services.

The collection of information by the Islamabad police regarding the political party's preparations extends to understanding the number of participants expected, the planned routes of marches, and the specific objectives of the protest. This intelligence helps in allocating resources appropriately and formulating a tailored response strategy to manage the event effectively.

The role of local administration, including the Deputy Commissioners and District Police Officers, is central to these preparations. They coordinate with various departments, including traffic police, intelligence agencies, and healthcare services, to ensure a unified response to any potential challenges posed by the protest.

Public safety announcements might also be issued closer to the date of the protest, advising citizens on potential road closures, alternative routes, and areas to avoid. These advisories are important for allowing residents to plan their daily activities and minimize inconvenience.

The administration's request for containers, as reported by police sources, indicates an expectation of significant mobilization by the PTI. Containers serve as a physical barrier, often deployed at entry and exit points of cities, around the Red Zone in Islamabad, or along major thoroughfares to control access and prevent protesters from reaching sensitive areas.

The overarching objective of these extensive preparations is to ensure that the protest, while allowing citizens their right to express dissent, does not escalate into violence or cause undue harm to public and private property. It also aims to minimize disruption to the daily lives of residents in the twin cities.

As September 27 approaches, the focus will remain on the implementation of these preventative measures and the response of both the protesting party and the public. The twin cities administrations are clearly poised to manage the situation with a comprehensive strategy, aiming for a peaceful resolution while maintaining law and order.
