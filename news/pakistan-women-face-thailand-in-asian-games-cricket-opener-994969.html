Pakistan Women Face Thailand in Asian Games Cricket Opener

By Umer Javed | 17 September 2026 | 3 min read
Photo: quang vinh / Pexels

Pakistan women’s cricket team begins Asian Games campaign against Thailand in Korogi Athletic Park, Aichi Prefecture, Japan. Fatima Sana leads squad after ACC Women’s Asia Cup win.

Pakistan women’s cricket team will face Thailand in their opening match at the Asian Games on Thursday at Korogi Athletic Park in Aichi Prefecture, Japan.

Opening Match on the Asian Games Stage

The match, scheduled for 10:30 a.m. local time, marks the start of Pakistan’s campaign in the 2026 Asian Games. The venue, a well‑known athletics ground in Nagoya, has hosted several international cricket events and offers a flat pitch that is expected to favor batters. The Pakistani side will look to capitalize on the momentum from their recent ACC Women’s Asia Cup triumph.

Recent ACC Women’s Asia Cup Victory

In the ACC Women’s Asia Cup held in Dubai, Pakistan defeated Thailand by 35 runs, securing a place in the final. Fatima Sana, the team’s captain, guided the side to a strong performance, with the bowlers containing the opposition and the batters building a solid foundation. The win reinforced Pakistan’s position as one of the top teams in the region.

Squad Adjustments and Player Absences

Wicket‑keeper Muneeba Ali will not travel to Japan, having returned home to care for her mother. Gull Feroza will replace her in the squad, bringing experience from domestic leagues. The rest of the team, including key players such as Sana and her vice‑captain, remain fit and ready for the continental tournament.

Journey to Japan

After the ACC Women’s Asia Cup, the Pakistan squad flew directly from the UAE to Nagoya, arriving two days before the opening game. The journey allowed the players to rest and acclimatise to the local climate. According to team officials, the squad used the downtime to review footage of Thailand’s recent matches, identifying strengths and weaknesses.

Expectations and Strategy

Pakistan’s coaching staff has emphasised the importance of a solid opening partnership. The team plans to use a balanced attack, with a mix of spin and pace to exploit the pitch conditions. The selectors have noted that the bowlers will aim to bowl tight lines and lengths, while the batters will focus on rotating the strike and building a steady run rate.

Historical Context of Pakistan Women’s Cricket

Pakistan women’s cricket has grown steadily since its inception in the early 2000s. The national team has participated in several ICC Women’s World Cups and the Asian Games, gradually improving its standing in the international arena. The 2026 Asian Games present an opportunity for the team to showcase its progress on a continental stage.

Thailand’s Cricket Profile

Thailand, a rising force in Asian women’s cricket, has made significant strides in recent years. The team’s recent performance in the ACC Women’s Asia Cup highlighted their ability to compete against stronger sides. Thailand’s bowlers have shown disciplined line and length, while their batters have displayed resilience under pressure.

Support and Fan Engagement

Both teams are expected to receive enthusiastic support from the local crowd. Pakistani fans in Japan have formed a small but vocal group, bringing flags and banners to cheer on their side. Thai supporters will also be present, creating a spirited atmosphere that reflects the competitive spirit of the Asian Games.

Looking Ahead

The outcome of the opening match will set the tone for Pakistan’s journey through the tournament. A win would give the team confidence and a positive start, while a loss would prompt a reassessment of tactics and strategy. The coaching staff remains focused on maintaining discipline and cohesion among players.

Concluding Remarks

As the Asian Games cricket tournament unfolds, Pakistan’s women’s team will aim to build on their recent success and deliver performances that reflect their dedication and skill. The match against Thailand will test their preparation and resolve, and the result will be closely watched by supporters across Pakistan and the region.
