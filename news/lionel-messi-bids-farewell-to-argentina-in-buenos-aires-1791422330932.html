An Emotional Night at the Monumental Stadium

Lionel Messi ended his international career with Argentina in the same manner that defined so much of his journey by creating goals, scoring, and offering his country a reason to celebrate. According to ARY News, the thirty-nine-year-old captain netted his one hundred and twenty-sixth international goal and provided two assists as Argentina defeated Benin three-to-zero in front of a crowd exceeding eighty-five thousand supporters at the Monumental on Tuesday night.

Nicolas Otamendi and Nico Paz contributed the other goals while Messi played a part in all three scoring actions. The outcome on the pitch was largely secondary to the broader significance of the evening. The event served as a formal farewell to a leading figure who has integrated into the national identity of Argentina. Weeks of anticipation preceded the fixture, which had been widely promoted as his final tango. By the time the player walked onto the turf, the atmosphere inside the venue was filled with intense emotion.

A massive Argentina jersey bearing the word gracias above the number ten was displayed across the pitch. Messi lined up for the national anthem accompanied by his three sons named Thiago, Mateo, and Ciro. Meanwhile, his wife Antonela Roccuzzo and his mother Celia Cuccittini observed the proceedings from the stands. The captain, usually known for his composed demeanor, fought back tears while the anthem played, and coach Lionel Scaloni showed clear signs of emotion as well.

Milestones and On-Field Brilliance

Play was temporarily halted in the tenth minute to honor the shirt number worn by Messi, prompting the crowd to rise in applause. Every subsequent touch of the ball by the captain drew another loud ovation from the spectators. Otamendi opened the scoring for Argentina following a chance created by Messi. Paz then extended the lead by converting another final pass supplied by the veteran playmaker.

Later in the match, during the seventy-first minute, Messi stepped forward to take a penalty kick and successfully converted it. This milestone marked his one hundred and twenty-sixth goal for the national team. Following the final whistle, a drone display illuminated the Buenos Aires sky with his likeness, and supporters continued singing in the stands long after the athletes had exited the pitch.

Reflecting on his departure, Messi stated that stepping away would be deeply painful and expressed a lifelong desire to play for the national team because representing Argentina is the most beautiful experience possible. Outlets including Dawn and Al Jazeera reported on the emotional atmosphere surrounding the farewell event, highlighting the roaring response from fans in Buenos Aires. Fox News noted that the celebrated player turned his final match into a gala performance by scoring one last time.

A Complex History With the National Team

Viewing the international career of Messi solely through its triumphs overlooks a much more complicated relationship with his home country during the earlier years. He made his senior debut back in two thousand and five under massive expectations. Argentina had previously produced legendary figures such as Diego Maradona, Gabriel Batistuta, Daniel Passarella, and Mario Kempes, and heavy demands were placed on Messi to follow in that tradition.

For many years, critics compared him directly to Maradona. His exceptional club form with Barcelona only intensified those comparisons. Whenever Argentina suffered defeats, the blame frequently fell upon him, and his commitment was questioned whenever the team fell short in finals. These struggles included a one-to-zero extra time loss to Germany in the two thousand and fourteen World Cup final. Following a defeat against Chile in two thousand and sixteen, Messi briefly announced his international retirement, though that decision lasted only sixty-six days.

A turning point arrived under the guidance of coach Scaloni, who assembled a younger and more balanced squad designed to share the heavy burden that had previously rested entirely on Messi. Close allies emerged within the team, including Rodrigo De Paul, Angel Di Maria, and Otamendi, who brought vital experience to the defensive line. This collective shift helped pave the way for a historic era of success for the national team.

Turning Tides and Major Trophies

The breakthrough for Messi and Argentina arrived at the two thousand and one Copa America tournament, where the team defeated Brazil one-to-zero in the final via a goal from Di Maria. This victory secured the first major senior international trophy for Messi, leading to memorable images of the captain sinking to the ground at the final whistle. The Finalissima victory against Italy followed in two thousand and twenty-two, setting the stage for the tournament in Qatar.

Argentina experienced a difficult start to the two thousand and twenty-two World Cup with a shock two-to-one defeat against Saudi Arabia. However, the team recovered by defeating Mexico, Poland, Australia, the Netherlands, and Croatia to reach the final match against France. That encounter became one of the greatest matches in tournament history. Messi scored twice, France forward Kylian Mbappe registered a hat-trick, and the contest was ultimately decided through a penalty shootout where Messi converted his spot-kick to secure the trophy.

That triumph ended a thirty-six-year wait for the World Cup trophy and cemented the legacy of Messi alongside Maradona in the football history of Argentina. He later added a second Copa America title in two thousand and four by defeating Colombia one-to-zero in the final. At thirty-nine years of age, Messi participated in his sixth World Cup during two thousand and six, scoring eight goals and guiding Argentina to another final following a dramatic semi-final win against England. Spain ultimately won that final one-to-zero, and Messi departed the pitch in tears.

Reflections From Teammates and Supporters

Following his final tournament appearance, Messi explained that he had given everything and that the next generation deserved an opportunity. He noted that the physical and emotional demands had been immense, compounded by the passing of his father and agent, Jorge Messi, just two months prior to the Benin match. Supporters gathered outside the Monumental stadium before the game carrying flags, shirts, and photographs to celebrate his career spanning more than two decades from two thousand and five to two thousand and six.

Tributes poured in from former teammates and coaching staff. Coach Scaloni remarked that he never imagined coaching the greatest footballer in history and noted that Messi leaves an incredible legacy. Neymar stated that the entire world thanks Messi for his contributions to the sport. Rodrigo De Paul described the captain as eternal, while Angel Di Maria expressed gratitude for being born in the same era. Cristian Romero and Nicolas Otamendi also shared messages of admiration and privilege regarding sharing the pitch with the legendary player.

For sports followers in Pakistan, the retirement of Messi marks the end of an era in global football. Many fans in Pakistan closely follow international tournaments and have watched his career evolve over two decades. Analysts suggest that the transition of leadership in the Argentina squad could reshape competitive dynamics in international football. Observers will be watching to see how the next generation of Argentine players performs in upcoming global competitions following the departure of their long-standing captain.
