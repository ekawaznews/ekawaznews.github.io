JI Chief Announces Islamabad March on Sept 20 Against Petroleum Levy

By Umer Javed | 16 September 2026 | 4 min read
Photo: Ann H / Pexels

JI chief has called for a march in Islamabad on September 20 to protest the new petroleum levy. The protest marks a new phase in the party’s campaign against rising fuel costs.

JI chief announces Islamabad march on Sept 20 against petroleum levy.

Jamaat-e-Islami’s Long‑Standing Opposition to Fuel Taxes

Jamaat-e-Islami, founded in 1941, has a history of opposing state policies it views as burdensome to ordinary citizens. The party’s base includes a mix of religious scholars, students and rural voters. Over the years, JI has staged rallies against various economic measures, citing the impact on the poor and middle class.

The Petroleum Levy and Its Political Fallout

The federal government introduced a petroleum levy in early August to increase revenue and curb consumption. The levy adds an extra 10 percent to the price of gasoline and diesel. Critics argue that the hike will further strain households already grappling with inflation. Supporters claim it will help finance infrastructure projects and reduce dependence on foreign oil.

Recent Protests and Public Sentiment

Fuel price hikes have sparked protests across major cities. In Karachi, Lahore and Peshawar, crowds gathered to demand the removal of the levy. The protests were largely peaceful, with demonstrators holding banners and chanting slogans. Police maintained order, but the crowds grew in size as the news of the levy spread.

JI’s Decision to March in Islamabad

According to Dawn, JI chief announced that the party would stage a march in the capital on September 20. The march is described as a “decisive phase” in the party’s campaign against the levy. The party intends to gather support from students, workers and ordinary citizens in the city.

Route and Organization of the Protest

Details of the march route have not yet been released. The party is reportedly coordinating with local student unions and trade unions to ensure a smooth procession. Organizers have emphasized the need for a peaceful demonstration, warning against any acts of violence that could undermine the protest’s legitimacy.

Government Response to the Planned Protest

Government officials have not issued a formal statement regarding the planned march. However, the Ministry of Finance has reiterated that the petroleum levy is essential for fiscal stability. The ministry also warned that any disruption to the national transport network could have economic repercussions.

Impact on the National Economy

Fuel prices directly affect transportation costs, which in turn influence the prices of goods across the country. A rise in fuel costs can lead to higher inflation, affecting food and commodity prices. The petroleum levy, while intended to boost revenue, could also dampen consumer spending.

Public Opinion and Social Media Reaction

Social media platforms have seen a surge in posts discussing the levy. Many users express frustration over the added cost, while others support the government’s fiscal measures. Hashtags such as #NoToPetrolTax and #FuelHike have trended on Twitter, reflecting the divided public sentiment.

Historical Context of Fuel Protests in Pakistan

Pakistan has a history of protests over fuel prices. In 2019, a nationwide strike by transport workers halted traffic in several cities. The government eventually offered concessions, but the protests highlighted the sensitivity of fuel policy in the country’s political.

Strategic Significance for JI

For JI, the march in Islamabad could serve multiple purposes. It offers a platform to rally its core supporters and to attract new voters disillusioned with the current administration. The party also aims to position itself as a champion of the people’s economic concerns.

Potential Outcomes of the Protest

Should the march proceed without incident, it could raise the profile of JI in the capital and potentially influence policy discussions. Conversely, any clashes with security forces could damage the party’s public image and lead to legal repercussions for participants.

Legal and Security Considerations

Under the Public Security Act, unauthorized gatherings can be declared unlawful. The police are expected to enforce crowd control measures, including the use of tear gas and rubber bullets if necessary. JI has called on its members to remain disciplined and to avoid any form of violence.

Economic Forecasts and Fuel Price Trends

Analysts predict that the petroleum levy will push gasoline prices up by approximately 20 percent. This increase could translate into higher transportation costs for businesses and consumers alike. The Ministry of Finance has projected a modest rise in revenue, but the net effect on the economy remains uncertain.

Political Ahead of September 20

The upcoming march comes at a time when opposition parties are consolidating their stance against the ruling coalition. The performance of JI in the protest could influence the party’s standing in the forthcoming general elections scheduled for 2025.

Conclusion: A Moment of Political Mobilization

The Islamabad march on September 20 marks a significant moment for Jamaat-e-Islami and the broader opposition movement. Whether the protest will achieve its objectives or provoke a larger political shift remains to be seen. The coming weeks will reveal how the government and the public respond to the escalating debate over the petroleum levy.
